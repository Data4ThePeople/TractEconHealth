# R048: Indianola and rural Sunflower County, with pieces of Bolivar, Leflore and Washington counties, MS

- 28133950600: Inverness and the Indianola fringe.

## Rule-out flags
- This is not one neighborhood. It is a 40-mile spread of farm towns across four counties.
- The boundaries shifted since 1990 (stability 0.763, "judgment call").
- Incarcerated people: 578 in Bolivar tract 9504 (15% of that tract) and 154 in Indianola tract 9504.02, 732 in all, or about 4% of the 2020 cluster (2020 PL, table P5). The facility names are UNCONFIRMED.
- The story overlaps heavily with Clarksdale (M016).

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1904: The state opened Parchman Farm on 20,000 acres in the county (https://mississippiencyclopedia.org/entries/sunflower-county/).
- 1930: The county had 66,364 people, the third most in the state. Of its 12,374 farms, 94% were run by tenants (Mississippi Encyclopedia, above).
- 1930 to 1960: The county lost nearly a third of its residents, falling to 45,750. Three-quarters of those who left were Black (above). The county had 25,971 people in 2020, 61% fewer than in 1930 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunflower_County,_Mississippi).
- July 11, 1954: The first Citizens' Council formed in Indianola (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizens%27_Councils, citing The Mississippi Encyclopedia).
- August 31, 1962: Fannie Lou Hamer, a sharecropper and timekeeper on the Marlow plantation near Ruleville, tried to register to vote and was fired. In 1969 she founded the Freedom Farm Cooperative (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fannie_Lou_Hamer; https://snccdigital.org/events/fannie-lou-hamer-founds-freedom-farm-cooperative/).
- 1960s to 2010: Indianola Academy, North Sunflower Academy and Central Delta Academy (Inverness) began as segregation academies. Central Delta closed in 2010 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunflower_County,_Mississippi, citing Moye, *Let the People Decide*).
- 1981 to 1990: Delta Pride, a catfish cooperative, grew to more than 1,000 jobs in Indianola. It was the county's largest employer by 1990. A 13-week strike ran from September to December 1990 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1990_Delta_Pride_strike).
- August 2011: Delta Pride closed its Indianola processing plant (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianola,_Mississippi, citing the Enterprise-Tocsin). In December 2011 county unemployment was 16.2% (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunflower_County,_Mississippi).

## Story strength (1-5)
4. The history is among the richest in the Delta (Citizens' Council, Hamer, Delta Pride), but the cluster is spread out, its boundaries shifted, and it overlaps with Clarksdale.

## What makes it distinct
It holds both sides of the civil rights fight in one county: where the Citizens' Council began and where Fannie Lou Hamer was fired. Then a catfish plant brought jobs and a landmark strike, and closed in 2011.
