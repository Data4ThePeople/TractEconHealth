## History (dated bullets with sources)

- **1900: county peak.** Iroquois County peaked at 38,014 and fell to 27,077 by 2020 (same page, from census).
- **2000: town peak.** Watseka grew until 2000 (5,670) and fell to 4,679 by 2020, a 17% drop (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watseka,_Illinois).
