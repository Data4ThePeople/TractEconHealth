# U037: Scattered tracts in four parishes around Monroe and Ruston (south Monroe, Richwood, south Ruston, Columbia, Chatham)

## Name and place
This cluster is not one neighborhood. Its tracts span four parishes and about 60 miles (TIGERweb place layer):
- Ouachita Parish: tracts 11 and 107 (Monroe, 94% to 96% Black), 110 (south Monroe and the town of Richwood), 106.04 (Richwood and rural south Ouachita)
- Lincoln Parish: tracts 9608 and 9609 (south Ruston, 70% to 80% Black)
- Caldwell Parish: tract 2 (Columbia, Grayson, Banks Springs; 72% white)
- Jackson Parish: tract 9701 (Chatham, Eros; 69% white)

Population about 23,481.

## Rule-out flags
- Not a coherent neighborhood: four parishes, two cities, and rural towns with different racial makeups and histories. Strong reason to rule out as a single spotlight.
- Tract 110 holds 211 people in group quarters, including 136 in adult corrections and 75 in juvenile facilities (2020 census), about 4% of the tract. The Ouachita Correctional Center near Richwood is likely, UNCONFIRMED. Not large enough to drive the numbers.
- No college housing in the Ruston tracts (Louisiana Tech is not in them).
- Overlaps with U026 for Monroe.
