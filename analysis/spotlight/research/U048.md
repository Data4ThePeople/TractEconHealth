## History (dated bullets with sources)

- **About 1937 to 1940: HOLC grades.** Our overlay of Mapping Inequality polygons (https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining): tract 2 is 89% grade D, tract 50 is 67% D, tract 9 is 50% D and 34% industrial. Most of the South Side was rated D.
- **1970: city peak.** Peoria reached 126,963 and fell to 113,504 by 1990 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peoria,_Illinois, from census).
- **Early 1980s: Caterpillar crisis.** Caterpillar came close to bankruptcy, losing almost $1 million per day at one point; layoffs led to strikes (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caterpillar_Inc.). Peoria-area layoff counts: UNCONFIRMED.
- **1992 and 1994 to 1995: strikes.** The UAW struck Caterpillar for five months in 1992, then over 10,000 members struck for 17 months in 1994 to 1995 and returned without a contract (Caterpillar Wikipedia page).
- **2017 to 2018: headquarters leaves.** Caterpillar announced Jan. 31, 2017, that it would move its headquarters to Deerfield, Ill. (about 300 headquarters positions), after more than 110 years in Peoria; it later moved to Irving, Texas, in 2022 (Caterpillar and Peoria Wikipedia pages).
- **Other closures** (Hiram Walker distillery, Pabst brewery, Keystone Steel and Wire cuts): UNCONFIRMED; not checked.
- **61605 incarceration.** The South Side ZIP code has been widely reported as having one of the highest incarceration rates in Illinois: UNCONFIRMED (source not checked).
