# U044: West Side Rochester, NY (Bull's Head, JOSANA, Edgerton, Plymouth-Exchange)

## Name and place
- Rochester's west side, Monroe County, NY: **Bull's Head**, **JOSANA**, **Edgerton**, **Dutchtown** and the **Genesee-Jefferson / Jefferson Avenue** area. OpenStreetMap reverse geocodes give Edgerton (tract 23), JOSANA (41) and Genesee-Jefferson (64, 65); tracts 96.01, 96.02, 96.05 sit around the Bull's Head intersection (centroids at 43.152-43.160, -77.630 to -77.641).
- No prison, base or college found. Former St. Mary's Hospital at Bull's Head is near tract 96 (status UNCONFIRMED).

## Rule-out flags
- **Too similar to U043**: same city, same Kodak and 1964 story; U043 has the stronger dated record. Keep one Rochester candidate.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1930s: Rochester HOLC map (grades UNCONFIRMED). https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining
- July 25, 1964: The uprising spread to the Third Ward on its second night (Third Ward lies just east of this cluster; overlap with tracts 64-65 UNCONFIRMED). https://rocwiki.org/1964_Race_Riot
- 1960s: Clearance of the Third Ward (Clarissa Street) and I-490 construction (UNCONFIRMED for these tracts; see https://www.clarissauprooted.org/?p=287).
- 1982 onward: Kodak's Rochester jobs fell from more than 60,000 (1982). https://rbj.net/2005/04/08/kodak-loses-title-as-top-employer/ Kodak Park sits northwest of this area.
- Decades to 2009: Bull's Head went from the city's western gateway to "significant disinvestment over several decades," tied to suburbanization and white flight. https://cityofrochester.gov/bullsheadrevitalization ; https://knowledge.uli.org/en/reports/aspr/2021/rochester-ny ; https://southwesttribune.com/?p=626
- 2009, 2017, 2018: City designated Bull's Head for urban renewal, expanded it, and adopted the plan July 24, 2018; about $10 million spent on 12 acres. https://cityofrochester.gov/news/city-announces-next-steps-revitalization-bulls-head-neighborhood ; https://www.wxxinews.org/local-news/2020-07-20/next-on-the-citys-agenda-finding-developers-for-bulls-head-neighborhood
- 2021: State designated Bull's Head a Brownfield Opportunity Area. https://dos.ny.gov/news/new-york-department-state-designates-city-rochesters-bulls-head-brownfield-opportunity-area-0
