# U065: West Garfield Park (K-Town area), Chicago (editor seed)

## Name and place

One tract, 17031260200, in West Garfield Park (community area 26) on Chicago's West Side, Cook County, Illinois, around Karlov Ave. near Madison and Lake streets. This is the "K-Town" area (streets named with K), listed on the National Register in 2010 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Garfield_Park,_Chicago). 2020 census population 1,054 (batch file 1,209). No group quarters.

## Rule-out flags

- Single small tract (about 1,000 to 1,200 people). Estimates for small tracts are noisy.
- Least extreme numbers of the three Chicago seeds (income pctile 14.4, health 13.5, median household income about $49,000).
- HOLC: 65% grade C, 34% grade D (our overlay), so the redlining case is weaker than Englewood or Woodlawn.
- Story is close to Englewood's. Recommend expanding to all of West Garfield Park if the editor wants the West Side.

## History (dated bullets with sources)

- **About 1940: HOLC grades.** 65% C (area C151, "Definitely Declining"), 34% D (Mapping Inequality overlay, https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining).
- **1950s: Eisenhower Expressway** built along the area's south side with a Blue Line median. Construction years and displacement: UNCONFIRMED.
- **Aug. 1965: West Garfield Park disturbance** after a fire truck killed a woman: UNCONFIRMED (not in the pages read).
- **April 5 to 7, 1968: riots.** Unrest spread along a 28-block stretch of West Madison St.; three miles of East and West Garfield Park along Madison were left in rubble; 2,150 arrests citywide; 210 buildings damaged; many lots remain vacant (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1968_Chicago_riots).
- **1970 to 1990: population loss.** 48,459 to 24,095, then 17,433 in 2020 (West Garfield Park Wikipedia page, from census).
- **2016: violence.** 31 murders, a rate of 172 per 100,000 (West Garfield Park Wikipedia page).
