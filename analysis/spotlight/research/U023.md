# U023: Fairhill and West Kensington, Philadelphia, PA ("the Badlands")

## Name and place
- **Fairhill** and western **Kensington / Harrowgate**, North Philadelphia, PA, informally "the Badlands." Fairhill runs Front St to 10th St, Cumberland St to Allegheny Ave; the Badlands runs Broad St to Kensington Ave, York St to Hunting Park Ave and largely matches the 25th police district. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairhill,_Philadelphia. Tracts 165, 174, 175, 176.01, 195.01, 200 fall in Fairhill/Hartranft; 177.02 and 192 sit east of Front St toward Kensington Ave. 2900 N 5th St geocodes to tract 175 (Census geocoder).

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1880s-1900s: Fairhill built as rowhouse worker housing, largely for German immigrants. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairhill,_Philadelphia
- Mid-1800s to early 1900s: Kensington one of the leading textile centers, especially carpets. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kensington,_Philadelphia
- 1937: HOLC map of Philadelphia (grades UNCONFIRMED). https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining
- 1950s-1960s: Deindustrialization took hold, with population loss, high unemployment and abandoned buildings; demographic change began in Fairhill. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kensington,_Philadelphia ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairhill,_Philadelphia (mill closing dates and job counts UNCONFIRMED)
- 1970s: El Centro de Oro Latino commercial district formed at 5th and Lehigh. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairhill,_Philadelphia
- 1990s-2007: The area held several of the city's top drug corners (3rd & Indiana ranked No. 2 in 2007); Operation Sunrise targeted the 25th and 26th districts (1998, UNCONFIRMED). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairhill,_Philadelphia
- 2010: Fairhill poverty rate 61%; ZIP 19133 median household income $14,185. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairhill,_Philadelphia
- 2017: Police cleared a large heroin encampment on abandoned rail tracks near Lehigh Ave (BBC, July 20, 2017). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kensington,_Philadelphia

## What makes it distinct
A Puerto Rican neighborhood in a former mill district that became the East Coast's largest open-air drug market across two drug eras.
