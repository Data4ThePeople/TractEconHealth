## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1941 to 1967: Roswell Army Air Field, later Walker Air Force Base, was the main employer; it closed July 1, 1967 (https://vintageaviationnews.com/aviation-museum-news/legacy-walker-air-force-base.html).
- 1966: A Federal Housing Administration market study forecast that the area would lose 13,350 nonfarm residents and 3,700 households in two years after the closing announcement (https://www.huduser.gov/portal/publications/pdf/scanned/scan-chma-RoswellNM-1966.pdf).
- 1960 to 1970: Roswell's population fell from 39,593 to 33,908, a 14.4% drop (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roswell,_New_Mexico).
- 1974 to 2002: A bus plant (Transportation Manufacturing Corp., later Nova Bus) ran in Roswell until it closed in 2002; successor Millennium Transit Services failed in 2012 (same source).
- 2000 to 2020: Hispanic share rose from 44.34% to 58.82% (same source).
- Oil and dairy: local economy depends on petroleum facilities and dairying; boom and bust dates UNCONFIRMED.
