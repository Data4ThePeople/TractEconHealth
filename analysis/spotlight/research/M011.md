# M011: Minden's Black neighborhoods, Doyline (Camp Minden) and north Bienville Parish, Louisiana

## Name and place
- Eight tracts, two parishes. The batch file labels this Webster Parish, but three tracts (22013970200, 970300, 970400) are in Bienville Parish.
- Webster Parish tracts (TIGERweb place layers, https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov):
  - 22119031700, 031800, 031900: inside the city of Minden. In 2020, 031700 was 83% Black (2,245 of 2,705) and 031900 was 83% Black (1,883 of 2,258); 031800 was 43% Black (2020 Census PL 94-171, https://api.census.gov/data/2020/dec/pl). These look like Minden's historically Black neighborhoods, which include the old Back Street business district (Webster Parish Journal, https://websterparishjournal.com/2022/09/29/historically-speaking-a-look-back-at-back-street/). Exact neighborhood names: UNCONFIRMED.
  - 22119031502: Doyline and Dixie Inn, west of Minden, which contains the former Louisiana Army Ammunition Plant, now Camp Minden.
  - 22119032101: rural land south of Minden toward Sibley and Dubberly.
- Bienville Parish tracts: 970200 (Gibsland, Arcadia, Bryceland, Mount Lebanon area, 61% Black), 970300 (Ringgold area), 970400 (Castor, Jamestown, Hall Summit area).

## Rule-out flags
- This is a sprawling mix: three urban Minden tracts plus five rural tracts across two parishes. It may work better as "Minden's Black neighborhoods" (three tracts) with Camp Minden as the nearby industrial backdrop.
- Group quarters are small: the largest are nursing facilities of 113 (031700) and 86 (031900), and 56 military in 031502 (2020 PL table P5). No single institution drives the numbers.
- Tract boundaries are stable since 1990 (stability 0.927).

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- Late 1800s to early 1900s: Minden grows as a railroad and lumber town; a Minden Lumber Company mill burned (date in source) (Webster Parish Journal, https://websterparishjournal.com/2026/06/02/historically-speaking-minden-lumber-company-burns/). Bienville Parish is described as an agricultural and timber parish (LSU Libraries, https://lib.lsu.edu/collections/digital/dlnp/newspaper-histories/Bienville-Democrat).
- 1941-1942: Construction of the Louisiana Ordnance Plant on nearly 15,000 acres west of Minden, mostly forest, fields and scattered homes and churches; it brought a housing shortage (Webster Parish Journal, https://websterparishjournal.com/2024/09/27/gorman-presents-plant-history-to-lions-club/ and https://websterparishjournal.com/2024/10/10/ordnance-plant-brings-housing-shortage/).
- 1951-1967: Reopened for the Korean War; employment reached 5,000 in 1953 and 7,800 in 1967 (Wikipedia, Louisiana Army Ammunition Plant, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_Army_Ammunition_Plant; figures need checking against primary sources).
- 1974-1979: Most plant operations ceased in 1974 (EPA document via search, https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2015-03/documents/1150056.pdf); employment was 1,700 in 1979 (Wikipedia, same page). Operated by Thiokol 1975-1989.
- 1989 (UNCONFIRMED date): The plant is placed on the EPA Superfund National Priorities List for explosives contamination of soil and groundwater (EPA Superfund site page, archived, https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20161207150721/https://cumulis.epa.gov/supercpad/cursites/csitinfo.cfm?id=0600770).
- 2005: Federal government transfers the site to the state; it becomes Camp Minden, a Louisiana National Guard training site (Wikipedia, same page).
- October 15, 2012: Explosion at the Explo Systems site shatters windows four miles away; investigators find millions of pounds of improperly stored M6 propellant, and Doyline is evacuated. Explo goes bankrupt and abandons the material (EPA fact sheet, https://www.epa.gov/sites/production/files/2015-03/documents/camp_minden_fact_sheet_march_2015_3.pdf; Claims Journal, https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/southcentral/2012/12/12/219032.htm).
- 2015-2016: After local opposition to open burning, a contained burn destroys the propellant (Biz New Orleans/AP, https://bizneworleans.com/camp-minden-to-burn-millions-of-pounds-of-explosive-material/).
- 2015-2025: Bienville Parish child poverty rises from 38% (2015-2019 ACS) to 52% (2020-2024 ACS) per USAFacts (https://usafacts.org/answers/what-is-the-us-poverty-rate/county/bienville-parish-la/); check against Census SAIPE.

## Story strength (1-5, with one sentence why)
3. The ammunition plant gives a strong, dated employment arc (7,800 jobs in 1967 to 1,700 in 1979 to near zero) plus a Superfund listing and the 2012 explosion, but tying those jobs to Minden's Black neighborhoods needs hiring records that I did not find.

## What makes it distinct
A federal war plant that built a town's economy for 30 years, wound down in the 1970s, then left behind contamination and an explosives crisis that forced an evacuation in 2012.
