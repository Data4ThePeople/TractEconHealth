## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1940s on: Henderson County expands from farming into manufacturing (Texas Historical Commission marker text, https://atlas.thc.texas.gov/Details/5213010357).
- 1955: A furniture plant is built in Athens; later Harvey Industries makes television cabinets there for Curtis Mathes (TCEQ, https://www.tceq.texas.gov/remediation/superfund/state/harvey.html).
- 1992: Harvey Industries goes through bankruptcy and abandons its water treatment plant; the site becomes a state Superfund site (TCEQ, same link).
- 1997: Hawn Lumber Company, a family firm central to the city's growth, closes (Texas Historical Commission marker, https://atlas.thc.texas.gov/Details/5213012010/print).
- Undated: WARN notices list closures at Heilig-Meyers Furniture (79 workers) and Central Garden and Pet (80 workers) in Athens (third-party tracker, https://warnfirehose.com/layoffs/texas/county/henderson). Dates UNCONFIRMED.
