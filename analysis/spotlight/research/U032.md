## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1930: Youngstown population 170,002; it stays near 167,000 through 1960 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youngstown,_Ohio).
- 1940 (approx.): HOLC grades 51% of the sampled area "D" and 38% "C" (Mapping Inequality, our point sample).
- 1950: Youngstown Sheet and Tube employs 27,000 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youngstown_Sheet_and_Tube).
- September 19, 1977 ("Black Monday"): Sheet and Tube closes the Campbell Works and furloughs 5,000 workers (same source).
- 1979 to 1980: Brier Hill Works closes (1979) and U.S. Steel leaves the area; Republic Steel goes bankrupt in the mid-1980s; the area loses an estimated 40,000 manufacturing jobs and 400 satellite businesses (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youngstown_Sheet_and_Tube; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youngstown,_Ohio).
- 2004 to 2005: Westlake Terrace is demolished and Arlington Heights rebuilt (begun 2004); the Youngstown 2010 plan (2005) plans for a smaller city (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youngstown,_Ohio).
- 2020: Population 60,068, down 65% from 1930 (same source).
- Crack era, foreclosure and opioid chapters: not yet sourced.
