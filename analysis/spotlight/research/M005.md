# M005: Crowley (north, west and south sides), Acadia Parish, LA

## Name and place
Four tracts in and around Crowley, the parish seat, which calls itself the "Rice Capital of the World" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowley,_Louisiana). By the TIGERweb places layer and tract extents:
- 22001960801: the east side, mostly white (2,798 of 3,593 people).
- 22001960901: the west-central side, 53% Black.
- 22001960902: the far west side and rural edge, 65% Black.
- 22001961000: the south side.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1886 to 1887: Brothers C.C. and W.W. Duson founded Crowley as a railroad town on the Louisiana Western line (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowley,_Louisiana).
- Early 1900s: Crowley became a major rice harvesting and milling center. It still has rice mills, and it hosts the International Rice Festival (above). Dates and mill counts are UNCONFIRMED.
- 1980s: Oil bust in Acadiana. The local effect is UNCONFIRMED in this pass.
- 1990 to 2024: Tract poverty rose from 39% in 1990 to 47% in 2020 to 2024. Vacancy rose from 12% (1990) to 21% (2020). Population fell from about 11,500 to 9,000 between 1990 and 2020 (project file history_long.csv).
- 2019: City median household income was $26,972, and 37.1% of residents were in poverty (ACS, cited at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowley,_Louisiana).
