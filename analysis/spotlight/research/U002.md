# U002: North Toledo / the North End (Vistula, Sherman, Polish Village, North River, Olde Towne), Toledo, OH

## Name and place
Eight tracts, population about 16,778, north of downtown Toledo on the west bank of the Maumee. City of Toledo neighborhood layer (Lucas County GIS): Industrial North (9), Polish Village (10.02), North River (12.02), Sherman (18, 19), Vistula (29, 30), Olde Towne (105) (https://lcaudgis.co.lucas.oh.us/gisaudserver/rest/services/Hosted/City_of_Toledo_Neighborhoods_/FeatureServer/0). Locals call the area North Toledo or the North End; Vistula is now part of the ONE Village area (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo,_Ohio).

## Rule-out flags
- No public housing complex found in these tracts in the HUD layer; no prison or college found.
- Libbey's glass factory (940 Ash St) sits inside tract 30 (Census geocoder). It is an employer, not a residential institution.
- Thin dated documentation of neighborhood-level decline so far.
- Boundaries stable (0.967).

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1833 to 1837: Vistula is platted in 1833 and merges with Port Lawrence to form Toledo in 1837 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vistula_Historic_District).
- 1938/1939: HOLC grades 41% of the sampled area "C" and 14% "D"; much is industrial and ungraded (Mapping Inequality, our point sample).
- 1942: Autolite opens the Stickney Ave plant, later a Jeep plant, in the tract next to this cluster (4000 Stickney Ave, tract 11) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo_Complex; Census geocoder).
- 1953: Willys-Overland moves its headquarters out of Toledo (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo,_Ohio).
- 1970 to 2020: Toledo population peaks at 383,818 in 1970 and falls to 270,871 (same source).
- 2006 to 2007: DaimlerChrysler ends production at the Parkway plant in 2006 and closes the Parkway and Stickney sites in 2007 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo_Complex). Job counts not found.
- August 2014: Algae bloom shuts off Toledo tap water for nearly three days (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toledo,_Ohio). Citywide, not distinct.
- I-75 and I-280 construction through the North End: dates and displacement UNCONFIRMED.

3. The industrial frame (Jeep, glass) is real and nearby, but neighborhood-level dates, job counts and freeway impacts still need sourcing.

## What makes it distinct
Toledo's oldest settlement and its Polish working-class district, wedged between glass and Jeep plants and the river, losing the plants one by one.
