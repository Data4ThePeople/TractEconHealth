# R028: East Prairie, Mississippi County, Missouri (Bootheel)

## Name and place
- One tract, 29133950400 (Census Tract 9504, Mississippi County), about 4.3 square miles of land. 2020 population 3,826 (2020 PL 94-171); candidate file pop 3,543.
- The centroid falls in the city of East Prairie, St. James Township (Census geocoder, https://geocoding.geo.census.gov). East Prairie had 2,943 people in 2020 (Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Prairie,_Missouri).

## Rule-out flags
- No prison, college or base. Group quarters are 29 people, mostly a nursing home (2020 PL 94-171 P5).
- Stable since 1990 (1.0).
- Too similar to R021 (Charleston), which is 12 miles away in the same county with the same cotton history and a stronger anchor event. This one's distinct piece is flooding and the floodway.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1883: Town founded as a stop on the St. Louis Southwestern ("Cotton Belt") Railway (Wikipedia, above; city history, https://eastprairiemo.net/history).
- 1930s: County cotton and sharecropping economy; county population peaks in 1940 at 23,149 (Wikipedia, Mississippi County, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi_County,_Missouri).
- 1937: Ohio-Mississippi flood. Refugees, many of them displaced sharecroppers, are housed in Red Cross boxcars in East Prairie and the high school becomes a hospital (St. Louis Public Radio, 2011, https://stlpr.org/government-politics-issues/2011-07-21/back-to-the-spillway-the-drama-of-the-flooding-secures-its-place-in-local-history).
- 1950s on: Congress authorizes closing the levee gap at the lower end of the New Madrid Floodway; the St. Johns Bayou and New Madrid Floodway project remains held up by funding and court challenges for decades (Corps of Engineers archived page, https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20090109054415/http:/www.mvm.usace.army.mil/StJohns/People_Community/default.asp).
- 1940-1970: County population falls to 16,647 as mechanization cuts farm labor (Wikipedia, Mississippi County).
- 1990s: East Prairie designated a federal Enterprise Community (Corps page, above). Year UNCONFIRMED.
- May 2-5, 2011: The Corps blasts the Birds Point levee and floods about 130,000 acres of the floodway in Mississippi and New Madrid counties to protect Cairo, Illinois; about 90 to 100 homes affected (NASA Earth Observatory, https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/images/50442/flooding-in-wake-of-levee-breach; Army.mil, https://www.army.mil/article/56150/engineers_blow_levees_to_protect_river_communities). Direct effect on East Prairie itself: UNCONFIRMED.
- 2010-2020: East Prairie falls from 3,176 to 2,943 (Wikipedia, above).

## Story strength (1-5, with one sentence why)
3. The cotton and flood history is dated, but East Prairie's own decline is thinly documented and its story overlaps heavily with Charleston.

## What makes it distinct
A cotton town inside the Mississippi's managed flood system, where federal flood control decisions (the unfinished levee gap and the 2011 levee blast) shaped farm land around it.
