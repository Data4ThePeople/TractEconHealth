# R032: South and west Madison, Madison County, FL (Madison Correctional Institution area)

## Name and place
Madison County tract 1103.02 (26.9 sq mi, ZIP 32340), covering the south and southwest parts of the city of Madison and nearby rural land. Madison Correctional Institution, a state men's prison at 382 SW MCI Way in Madison, opened in 1989 ([Florida DOC](https://fdc.myflorida.com/ci/216.html); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Correctional_Institution_(Florida))). Population 5,176 (ACS); 5,530 in 2020.

## Rule-out flags
- **Prison:** the 2020 census counted 1,366 people in correctional facilities in this tract, about 25% of residents. That is a large enough share to drive income and health numbers on its own. Recommend ruling out.
- **Boundary:** a piece of a larger 1990 tract.

## History
- **1827-1910s:** Madison County grew on Sea Island cotton; Madison was called the "Cotton Capital of Florida" ([Greene Publishing](https://www.greenepublishing.com/cotton-and-the-gin-how-madison-once-held-a-world-record/)).
- **1910s:** The boll weevil wiped out cotton within a few years; farmers moved to tobacco ([Greene Publishing](https://www.greenepublishing.com/tobacco-in-madison-county/)). Exact year UNCONFIRMED.
- **Late 1900s-2000s:** Tobacco faded with falling demand, litigation and labor costs ([Greene Publishing](https://www.greenepublishing.com/tobacco-in-madison-county/)). Dates UNCONFIRMED.
- **1989:** Madison Correctional Institution opened ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madison_Correctional_Institution_(Florida))).
