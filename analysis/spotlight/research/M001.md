## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1940 to 1980: Talladega grows from 9,298 to a peak of 19,128 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talladega,_Alabama).
- 1980 to 2020: The city falls to 15,861, a 17.1% drop. It loses 16.7% from 1990 to 2000 alone (18,175 to 15,143) (same source).
- 2000 to 2020: The Black share of the population goes from 42.3% to 52.2%, and the white share from 56.2% to 42.6%. In 2010, 25.5% of residents were in poverty, up from 19.0% in 2000 (same source).
