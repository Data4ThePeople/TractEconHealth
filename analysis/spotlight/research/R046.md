# R046: Pine Ridge village, Wounded Knee and Wanblee (Pine Ridge Reservation), with St. Francis and Mission (Rosebud Reservation), SD

## Name and place
Seven tracts across five counties, joined into one cluster by the screen. Population 27,038 (ACS).
- **Pine Ridge Reservation (Oglala Lakota Oyate, the Oglala Sioux Tribe):** Oglala Lakota County 9410 (Pine Ridge village, the tribal seat; Wakpamni District) and 9411 (Wounded Knee District); Jackson County 9412 (Wanblee, Eagle Nest District); Bennett County 9412 (LaCreek District, on reservation land; the population-weighted centroid falls here).
- **Rosebud Reservation (Sicangu Lakota Oyate, the Rosebud Sioux Tribe):** Todd County 9401 (St. Francis) and 9402 (Mission, Antelope).
- **Mellette County 9403:** inside the 1889 Rosebud boundary that the 1910 act opened (see below); about 58% American Indian in 2020.
Town placement is from the Census geocoder. Kyle (Oglala Lakota 9412) and Martin (Bennett 9410) are not in the cluster. The editor's seed is "Pine Ridge, SD," and Pine Ridge village is in tract 9410.

## Rule-out flags
- **Two nations in one cluster.** Pine Ridge and Rosebud are separate reservations with separate tribal governments. Recommend narrowing the story to the four Pine Ridge tracts (and possibly adding Kyle, 9412), or telling it as Pine Ridge only and dropping Todd and Mellette.
- Group quarters in 9410: 239 correctional (tribal jail in Pine Ridge village) and 118 college (Oglala Lakota College is UNCONFIRMED as the source). Small next to 27,000 people; not a reason to rule out.
- Stability 0.882 to 1990. Usable, but check Mellette and Bennett pieces.
- Series likely includes at most one reservation. This is the pick.

## History
- **1868:** The Fort Laramie Treaty set up the Great Sioux Reservation, about 60 million acres of western South Dakota including the Black Hills ([SERC, Carleton College](https://serc.carleton.edu/research_education/nativelands/pineridge/reservation.html); [SD State Historical Society](https://history.sd.gov/Archives/forms/indian%20archives/Indian%20Archives%20Project/greatsioux.pdf)).
- **1877:** The United States took the Black Hills after the 1876 war ([SERC](https://serc.carleton.edu/research_education/nativelands/pineridge/reservation.html)).
- **March 2, 1889:** Congress broke up the remaining reservation, taking about 11 million more acres and creating separate reservations, including Pine Ridge and Rosebud ([SD State Historical Society](https://history.sd.gov/Archives/forms/indian%20archives/Indian%20Archives%20Project/greatsioux.pdf); [Rosebud Sioux Tribe v. Kneip](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/430/584/)).
- **1890:** The Wounded Knee Massacre, in what is now Oglala Lakota County, within tract 9411's Wounded Knee District ([Wikipedia, Oglala Lakota County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oglala_Lakota_County,_South_Dakota)). The exact day (December 29) is not on that page.
- **1904-1910:** Acts opened Rosebud's unallotted land in Gregory (1904), Tripp and Lyman (1907) and Mellette (1910) counties to non-Indian settlers; the Supreme Court held in 1977 that these acts shrank the reservation ([Rosebud Sioux Tribe v. Kneip, 430 U.S. 584](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/430/584/)).
- **May 27, 1910:** Congress authorized sale of surplus Pine Ridge land in Bennett County to non-Indian settlers; a 1936 order restored undisposed land to the tribe ([Cook v. Parkinson, 8th Cir.](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/525/120/169809/); [Putnam v. U.S., 1957](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/248/292/328382/)). Bennett's population rose from 1,924 in 1920 to 4,590 in 1930 ([Wikipedia, Bennett County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bennett_County,_South_Dakota)).
- **1948-1973:** The BIA relocation program moved Native people from reservations to cities such as Denver; an estimated 100,000 relocated nationally from 1952 to 1973 ([KUNC](https://www.kunc.org/arts-life/2013-05-06/defunct-relocation-policy-still-impacts-american-indians); [Plains Humanities](https://plainshumanities.unl.edu/encyclopedia/doc/egp.na.122.html)). Pine Ridge counts not found.
- **1980-2014:** Oglala Lakota (then Shannon) County had the lowest U.S. life expectancy in IHME's county series for every year since 1980; 66.8 years in 2014 ([The Incidental Economist, citing IHME](https://theincidentaleconomist.com/wordpress/oglala-lakota-county-sd/)). The 2000 census put 52.3% of residents below poverty with median household income of $20,916 ([Wikipedia, Oglala Lakota County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oglala_Lakota_County,_South_Dakota)).
- **Also relevant, not sourced in this pass:** 1934 Indian Reorganization Act constitution; 1973 occupation at Wounded Knee; land fractionation and the Land Buy-Back Program; Whiteclay, Nebraska alcohol sales ending in 2017. All UNCONFIRMED here.
