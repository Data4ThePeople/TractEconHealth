# R036: Perry County (Marion, Uniontown), rural Dallas County (Orrville, Selmont/Craig Field) and southern Bibb County (Brent), AL

## Name and place
Eight rural tracts, population about 19,811, that span three counties around (but not including) Selma: Perry County tracts 6870.01, 6870.02 and 6871 (Marion area and Uniontown area; Uniontown placement in 6871 UNCONFIRMED), Dallas County tracts 9569 (west, Orrville area, UNCONFIRMED), 9571 and 9573.01 (southeast of Selma; 9573.01 centroid at 32.35, -86.99 matches the former Craig Air Force Base, now Craig Field), and Bibb County tracts 0100.09 and 0100.10 (Brent/Centreville area).

## Rule-out flags
- PRISON: Bibb Correctional Facility (565 Bibb Ln, Brent) geocodes to tract 01007010009 and has held about 1,880 men (https://www.mentalfloss.com/geography/9-facts-about-bibb-county-alabama; dated figure). This can skew that tract. Drop the two Bibb tracts.
- The cluster mixes three stories (Black Belt Perry County, a closed air base, a timber/prison town in Bibb). It reads as a patchwork.
- Stability 0.933 since 1990.
- Selma itself is not in the cluster, despite the label.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1930s to 1960s: Perry and Dallas were plantation cotton counties; Marion and Selma were centers of the voting rights movement in 1965 (general; specific citation for Perry needed, UNCONFIRMED).
- September 30, 1977: Craig Air Force Base near Selma closed after post-Vietnam cuts; a DTIC table lists 547 jobs lost in 1977 and local reports cite a payroll over $30 million (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_Air_Force_Base; https://www.waka.com/?p=90470; https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/ADA229443.pdf).
- 2002 to 2003: Uniontown residents opposed the Arrowhead Landfill permit, sited on a former plantation (https://earthjustice.org/feature/photos-a-toxic-inheritance).
- 2010: About 4 million cubic yards of coal ash from the 2008 TVA Kingston spill were shipped to Arrowhead; Perry County received fees estimated at $4.1 million (https://m.washingtontimes.com/news/2014/aug/14/epa-investigating-west-alabama-landfill/; https://earthjustice.org/feature/photos-a-toxic-inheritance). Years differ by source.
- April 27, 2011: A tornado hit Brent (Bibb), killing five (https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brent,_Alabama).
- July 31, 2021: Judson College in Marion closed after 183 years, citing low enrollment and money (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judson_College_(Alabama); https://baptistnews.com/article/judson-college-will-close-and-file-for-chapter-11-bankruptcy/).
