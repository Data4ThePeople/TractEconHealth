# R026: Bennettsville (south and east side), Marlboro County, SC

## Name and place
Single tract 45069960201, 5.6 square miles, population about 3,690, on the edge of Bennettsville, the Marlboro County seat. The centroid reverse-geocodes near Shuford Street (Dundee Acres area, OpenStreetMap). The federal prison FCI Bennettsville (696 Muckerman Rd) geocodes to tract 9603.02, so it is NOT in this tract.

## Rule-out flags
- Tract boundaries: piece of a larger 2010 tract; compare on the old outline or skip. No 1990 match.
- The textile story here is less documented than Bladen (R035) in what I found.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1897: D.D. McColl founded the Bennettsville Cotton Mill (https://sccounties.org/sites/default/files/uploads/resources/marlboro_0.pdf).
- Before 1900s: Cotton made Marlboro a wealthy county (same source). Mechanization and out-migration timing UNCONFIRMED.
- 1990s to 2000s: Textile plant closures in Marlboro (names, years, jobs) UNCONFIRMED.
- 2010 to 2020: Bennettsville's population fell from 9,069 to 7,020 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bennettsville,_South_Carolina; part of the change may reflect the prison count or boundaries, UNCONFIRMED).
- 2022: Mohawk closed its Bennettsville carpet yarn plant, 600 jobs, about a quarter of the county's manufacturing jobs (https://www.dew.sc.gov/news/2022-08/dew-working-get-mohawk-employees-jobs-they-are-unemployed).
- 2025: Bennettsville Printing announced a 24-job expansion (https://www.areadevelopment.com/newsitems/3-24-2025/bennettsville-printing-bennettsville-south-carolina.shtml).
