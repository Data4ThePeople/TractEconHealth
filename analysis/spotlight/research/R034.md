## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1920: Lanier County formed from parts of Berrien, Clinch and Lowndes (https://www.georgiaencyclopedia.org/articles/counties-cities-neighborhoods/lanier-county/).
- 1925 or 1928: Milltown, a mill village, was renamed Lakeland when it incorporated (same source; dates differ).
- 1987 to 1997: Hog farms in the county fell from 23 to 7 (Census of Agriculture, https://agcensus.library.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/1997-Georgia-County-Table-86.txt).
- 2014: Louis Smith Memorial Hospital passed to the Valdosta-Lowndes hospital authority and became SGMC Lanier Campus (https://sgmc.org/lanier-health-services-inc-to-transfer-ownership-to-hospital-authority-of-valdosta-lowndes-county-ga).
- Top employers include Moody Air Force Base (nearby) and the Department of Corrections (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lanier_County,_Georgia).
