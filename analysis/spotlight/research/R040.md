# R040: Pana, Illinois

## Name and place

One tract, 17021958900, the city of Pana, Christian County, Illinois (tract internal point is in Pana). 2020 census population 2,554 (Pana city 5,199, so the tract is part of the town). 63 nursing home residents; no prison (Taylorville Correctional Center is in Taylorville, not this tract), college or base.

## Rule-out flags

- Single tract, part of a small town.
- Thin documentation of decline after 1960. Not graded by HOLC.

## History (dated bullets with sources)

- **Late 1800s to early 1900s: coal town.** Pana was a coal mining center at a rail crossing (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pana,_Illinois).
- **April 1899: mine war.** In a labor fight, mine owners brought in Black miners from Alabama; seven people were killed in the April 1899 riot, and 211 Black residents later left for Weir, Kansas (same page).
- **"City of Roses":** Pana once had 109 greenhouses (same page). Years the rose industry rose and fell: UNCONFIRMED.
- **1960: peak.** 6,432 people, down to 5,199 in 2020 (same page, from census). Christian County peaked at 38,816 in 1950 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_County,_Illinois).
- **Mine closures** and later plant closures: UNCONFIRMED.

A coal and rose-greenhouse town with a violent 1899 mine war in its past.
