## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1917: Swift and Company bought the Moultrie packing plant (opened 1914); it employed more than 700 people at its 1950s peak (https://www.moultriega.com/things-to-do/attractions-activities/swift-company/).
- 1940s: Moultrie was mainly agricultural, with about 16,000 residents and about 4,000 Black residents (https://museumofeducation.info/moultrie.html).
- 1970: Swift moved operations to a new plant (city site above).
- 1996: Premium Pork, which bought the Swift plant, closed it (https://fieldreport.caes.uga.edu/news/pork-processor-reopening/). Job count UNCONFIRMED.
- 2001: National Beef opened a plant in Moultrie and later expanded (https://www.areadevelopment.com/newsItems/11-14-2017/national-beef-packing-company-production-plant-moultrie-georgia.shtml).
