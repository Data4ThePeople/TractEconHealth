# U038: West Decatur (along Moulton Street West), Decatur, AL

## Name and place
- Morgan County, AL. 2 tracts (01103000600, 01103000700), 4.8 sq mi, pop 6,721. Centroid 34.6035, -87.0098, west of downtown Decatur.
- 1000 Moulton St W geocodes to tract 000600 (https://geocoding.geo.census.gov). Wikipedia puts "West Decatur north of Moulton Street" in the city's Northwest region and "West Decatur south of Moulton Street, along with Moulton Heights" in the Southwest region (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decatur,_Alabama). Tract 000600 is north of Moulton and 000700 is south, so the local name is "West Decatur," possibly including Moulton Heights. UNCONFIRMED.
- Institution: tract 000600 holds the North Alabama Community Based Facility, a state work release center at 1401 Highway 20 West. The geocoder places that address in tract 000600. The 2020 census counts 683 people in adult correctional facilities there, 20.9% of the tract and 10.2% of the cluster (PL 94-171 table P5). The facility is minimum custody and opened in May 1981. Bed counts vary by source (680 to 740) (https://doc.alabama.gov/facaddr.aspx; https://prisonersofthecensus.org/data/prisons2020/AL).

## Rule-out flags
- A state work release center holds about 10% of the cluster's people. Work-release inmates can affect income and health model estimates. Rule out, or rebuild the cluster without tract 000600.
- Boundaries are stable (1.0, back to 1970).

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1971-2018: Brookhaven Middle School serves west Decatur. It closes in 2018, and the school board sells the site to 3M for $1.25 million after PFAS contamination is found (https://news.3m.com/heres_what_to_know_Brookhaven).
- 1960s-2002: The 3M Decatur plant makes PFOA and PFOS until it phases them out in 2002. Waste reaches the Tennessee River and leaks from landfills (https://pfasproject.com/decatur-alabama/; https://archive.epa.gov/region4/water/documents/web/pdf/epa_decatur_fact_sheet_final.pdf). Whether the contamination reaches these two tracts is UNCONFIRMED.
- 1981: The Decatur Work Release Center opens on Highway 20 West (https://doc.alabama.gov/facaddr.aspx; per search summary).
- 1933: The Scottsboro Boys retrials are held in Decatur. Widely documented, but no source was pulled. UNCONFIRMED.
- Plant closings (for example, Wolverine Tube), years and job counts: not found. UNCONFIRMED.
- HOLC map for Decatur: none known. UNCONFIRMED.
