# U064: Grove Hall, Boston (Roxbury/Dorchester line)

## Name and place
- **Grove Hall**, Boston, Suffolk County, MA. Two tracts along Blue Hill Avenue: 821 (Roxbury side, around Schuyler St) and 901 (Dorchester side; 560 Blue Hill Ave, 60 Wolcott St and 100 Columbia Rd all geocode here via the Census geocoder). The Roxbury-Dorchester line runs between Blue Hill Ave and Columbia Rd, and the area's name is contested between the two (https://baystatebanner.com/?p=58254). Matches editor seeds Roxbury and Dorchester.
- Institutions: Franklin Field Apartments (91 Ames St) geocodes to tract 1001, outside the cluster. No prison, base or college found in the two tracts. Public housing inside 821/901 not yet checked.

## Rule-out flags
- None. Tracts are stable since 1970.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1930s: HOLC map of Boston; southern Blue Hill Avenue blocks "just barely escaped redlining" while blocks north were graded red. https://medium.com/timeline/when-this-group-of-black-mothers-locked-themselves-in-a-government-office-boston-erupted-in-riots-eaad1e64b92d ; https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining
- 1950s-60s: Black families moved south along Blue Hill Ave into a largely Jewish district; by 1967 the strip was called "Civil Rights Row." https://medium.com/timeline/when-this-group-of-black-mothers-locked-themselves-in-a-government-office-boston-erupted-in-riots-eaad1e64b92d
- June 1-2, 1967: Mothers for Adequate Welfare sat in at the welfare office at 515 Blue Hill Ave; police moved in and about three dozen businesses along a 15-block strip were looted or burned over two nights. https://whenandwhereinboston.org/?p=68071 ; https://thecrimson.com/article/1967/6/15/roxbury-quiet-in-past-finally-breaks ; https://hubhistory.com/episodes/fifteen-blocks-of-rage-episode-140
- April 1968: Further unrest after the King assassination (mentioned in sources; details UNCONFIRMED).
- 1968-1972: The Boston Banks Urban Renewal Group (B-BURG, formed 1963, revived 1968) steered FHA-insured loans for Black buyers into a drawn zone of Dorchester and Mattapan; blockbusting and fast turnover followed, and banks refused to reform the program. https://dotnews.com/node/140023 ; https://www.bu.edu/partisanreview/books/PR1992V59N3/HTML/files/assets/basic-html/page97.html ; https://bostonplans.org/getattachment/27777e46-3b89-4537-a057-c8a653415320 (foreclosure counts UNCONFIRMED; see Levine and Harmon, *The Death of an American Jewish Community*, 1992)
- 1974 onward: Court-ordered busing in Boston schools (UNCONFIRMED link to this tract).
- 1980s-1990s: Crack-era violence on Blue Hill Ave (UNCONFIRMED; needs local source).
- 2007-2010: Foreclosure wave in Dorchester/Roxbury (UNCONFIRMED; check HMDA and Warren Group data).
- 2013: Emmanuel Gospel Center study called the 1967 riot pivotal to the neighborhood. https://www.egc.org/blog-2/tag/Grove+Hall
