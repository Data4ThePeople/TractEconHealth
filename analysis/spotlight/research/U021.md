# U021: One tract in northeast Independence, Missouri (Kansas City area)

## Name and place
- One tract, 29095011405 (Census Tract 114.05, Jackson County), inside the city of Independence (Census geocoder, https://geocoding.geo.census.gov). About 0.37 square miles of land, 2020 population 2,886.
- Bounding box roughly 39.106 to 39.119 N and -94.384 to -94.373 W, about 1 mile northeast of Independence Square (TIGERweb, https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov). Neighborhood name residents use: UNCONFIRMED.
- Housing is mostly rental apartments: of about 1,124 units, 346 are single detached houses and most of the rest are in 2-9 unit buildings; about 861 households rent; median year built 1974; about half of residents are below poverty (ACS 2019-2023 tables B25024, B25003, B25035, B17001, https://api.census.gov/data/2023/acs/acs5).

## Rule-out flags
- Tract boundaries: the stability note says this tract is a piece of a larger 1990 tract, so the history would have to be shown on the old tract's outline (stability 0.0, no oldest-year value).
- Single tract dominated by 1970s-era apartment complexes. The numbers may reflect who these complexes house rather than a long neighborhood decline.
- No group quarters (2020 PL 94-171 P5).
- Thin documentation: I found nothing written about this specific area.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1930s HOLC: Independence may fall at the edge of the Greater Kansas City map; check Mapping Inequality (https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining). UNCONFIRMED.
- 1970s: Most housing in the tract built around then (ACS B25035 median year built 1974, above).
- 1997: Jackson County logs 300 meth lab busts; Independence is called the center of the problem and the area is nicknamed "Meth Capital of the World"; seven highways cross or border the city (Media Awareness Project archive of local news, https://rave.ca/en/news_info/150140/all and https://rave.ca/en/news_info/150120/all).
- Late 2000s: Lab seizures fall sharply after the late 1990s; Jackson County had 21 seizures in 2009 (KTAR/AP, https://ktar.com/national-news/more-meth-labs-showing-up-in-cities-suburbs/111991/?nid=64).
- 2018: KCUR reports meth use growing again even as home labs disappear (https://www.kcur.org/health/2018-09-05/while-opioids-grab-the-spotlight-the-meth-epidemic-grows-worse-than-ever).
- Tract 114.05 appears on a Missouri list of tracts with poverty 30% or higher and unemployment 6.5% or higher (Missouri DHSS, https://health.mo.gov/safety/cannabis/pdf/mb-poverty-unemployment-qualified-census-tracts.pdf).
