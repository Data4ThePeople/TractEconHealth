# R003: Williamson and the upper Tug Valley, WV/KY/VA (Williamson, Matewan, Delbarton, Phelps, Hurley)

## Name and place
A rural cluster along the Tug Fork. In Mingo County, West Virginia: tract 9574 (the city of Williamson, the county seat; 8.9 sq mi), 9573 (Delbarton area), 9575 (Red Jacket and Matewan area) and 9576. Across the river: Pike County, Kentucky tracts 9317 and 9318 (Phelps/Freeburn area), Buchanan County, Virginia tract 102 (Hurley area), and McDowell County, WV tract 9539. Town names come from tract centroids and the Census geocoder and are approximate. Population 22,499.

## Rule-out flags
- No prison, college or base (group quarters are under 105 per tract, nursing homes).
- Three states and four counties. The Mingo County tracts carry the story.
- Strongly overlaps with R019 (lower Tug Fork, which includes Kermit) and R007 (McDowell). Pick one central Appalachian coal story. If R007 is chosen, this one should go.

## History
- **May 19, 1920:** The Matewan Massacre, a gunfight between miners and mine-guard detectives, part of the West Virginia Mine Wars. Date is from common history; source not pulled here (UNCONFIRMED; e-WV has an entry).
- **1950s-1980s:** Coal mechanization and later surface mining cut jobs. Job counts UNCONFIRMED; MSHA data can show them.
- **April 2-5, 1977:** The Tug Fork flood, the largest recorded in southern West Virginia, hit Williamson and Matewan. A 1974 Army Corps statement had called Matewan's flood problem the most severe in the country ([USGS Hydrologic Atlas 588](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ha588); [Clio](https://theclio.com/entry/20246)).
- **1991 and 1997:** The Army Corps finished floodwalls at Williamson ($41 million) and Matewan ([Clio](https://theclio.com/entry/20246)). Dates per Clio; check against Corps records.
- **2006-2016:** Distributors shipped 20.8 million hydrocodone and oxycodone pills to two pharmacies in Williamson, a town of about 3,200 ([House Energy and Commerce hearing record](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-115hhrg30665/html/CHRG-115hhrg30665.htm); [Press Herald/AP](https://www.pressherald.com/2018/01/31/20-8-million-pain-pills-sent-by-drug-makers-to-small-town/)).
- **2010s:** Coal jobs fell further as Central Appalachian production fell. County numbers UNCONFIRMED; EIA and MSHA have them.
- **Williamson Memorial Hospital:** possible financial failure or closure in the 2020s. UNCONFIRMED; not found in this search.

A small county seat was hit in turn by the Mine Wars, a 100-year flood that led to a floodwall around downtown, and a pill-shipment total for two pharmacies that became a congressional exhibit.
