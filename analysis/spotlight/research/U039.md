# U039: West Baltimore (Sandtown-Winchester, Harlem Park, Upton, Poppleton), MD

## Name and place
- **West Baltimore**, Baltimore city, MD: **Sandtown-Winchester** (tract 1501), **Harlem Park** (1601, 1603, 1604), **Upton** (1702, 1703), **Poppleton / Franklin Square** (1901, 2806). Tracts identified from TIGERweb centroids; 1525 Presstman St geocodes to 1501, 1201 N Fulton Ave and 1700 W Mosher St to 1603, 1200 W Baltimore St to 2806 (Census geocoder). The Franklin-Mulberry "Highway to Nowhere" runs between Harlem Park/Upton to the north and Franklin Square/Poppleton to the south (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_170_(Maryland)).
- Gilmor Homes (public housing, Sandtown) is likely in tract 1501 (UNCONFIRMED). It is one complex among eight tracts and does not drive the cluster by itself.

## Rule-out flags
- None. Tracts stable since 1970. The area is heavily covered nationally, which makes fresh angles harder.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1937: HOLC graded West Baltimore (expected D; UNCONFIRMED until checked). https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining
- 1950s-60s: Sandtown was a center of Black culture, sometimes called Baltimore's "Harlem." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandtown-Winchester,_Baltimore
- April 1968: Riots after the King assassination; cited among causes of later decline. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandtown-Winchester,_Baltimore
- 1975-1979: I-170 (Franklin-Mulberry Expressway) built through the area, demolishing homes; it was then cut off from the Interstate system in 1981-1983. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_170_(Maryland) (about 971 homes and 62 businesses demolished, UNCONFIRMED)
- 1990s: Sandtown-Winchester neighborhood transformation effort with Enterprise and the city (about $130 million, UNCONFIRMED).
- April 2015: Freddie Gray arrested in Sandtown; at the time about 3% of residents were incarcerated, a third of housing abandoned, and 20% of working-age adults unemployed. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandtown-Winchester,_Baltimore
- 2015: Red Line light rail, planned for the highway median, canceled; revived 2023. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_170_(Maryland)
