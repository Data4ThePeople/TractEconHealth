# U053: West Arlington (south of the Arlington Expressway), Jacksonville, FL

## Name and place
Duval County tract 155.02 (0.9 sq mi, ZIP 32211), inside the consolidated City of Jacksonville. From the tract bounding box (about Cesery Boulevard east toward Regency, between the Arlington Expressway and Atlantic Boulevard), the area is part of West or Old Arlington, the postwar suburb east of the St. Johns River. The exact local name of this pocket is UNCONFIRMED. The tract is a federal Opportunity Zone ([OpportunityZones.com](https://opportunityzones.com/?p=27986)). Population 5,502 (ACS); 5,077 in 2020 (49% Black, 19% Hispanic).

## History
- **April 1953:** The Mathews Bridge opened; for about 20 years Arlington was Duval County's fastest-growing area ([The Jaxson](https://thejaxsonmag.com/article/neighborhoods-of-jacksonville-monterey)).
- **1950s-1960s:** Developer Cesery built much of postwar Arlington, including hundreds of apartments ([The Jaxson, Arlington Heights](https://thejaxsonmag.com/article/neighborhoods-of-jacksonville-monterey-page-3)).
- **1967:** Regency Square Mall opened at the corridor's east end ([The Coastal](https://thecoastal.com/featured/brief-history-arlington/)).
- **1980s-2000s:** Growth moved to newer suburbs such as Baymeadows, and the expressway corridor aged ([Metro Jacksonville](https://archive.metrojacksonville.com/article/2013-jan-the-evolution-of-the-arlington-expressway)). Dates UNCONFIRMED.
- **2010s:** West Arlington described as blighted; the city adopted an Old Arlington neighborhood action plan ([The Coastal](https://thecoastal.com/featured/brief-history-arlington/); [City of Jacksonville](https://jacksonville.gov/departments/office-of-economic-development/docs/cras/old-arlington-nap-adopted-complete.aspx)). Plan date UNCONFIRMED.
