## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1920: Red River County population peaked at 35,829; cotton peaked at 46,263 bales, the most in any census year (https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/red-river-county).
- By 1930: fewer than 30% of farmers owned any of their land (tenancy and sharecropping) (same source).
- 1931 to 1936: Rated businesses in Clarksville fell from 165 to 104 as low cotton prices and the boll weevil hit the cotton trade (https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/clarksville-tx-red-river-county).
- 1930 to 1970: County population fell to 14,298; by 1982 cotton was down to 845 bales (https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/red-river-county). After WWII, urban flight and farm decline set in; county population fell by half from 1920 to 1980 (Clarksville entry above).
- 1980 to 1988: Young v. Pierce (class action from Clarksville plaintiffs) found HUD knowingly funded segregated public housing in 36 East Texas counties (1985). A separate ruling found the Clarksville Housing Authority assigned tenants by race. In 1988 its three sites were 33%, 48% and 89% Black (https://tarlton.law.utexas.edu/william-wayne-justice/young-case; https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp/685/986/1882294/). The case ended in 2004 (https://clearinghouse.net/case/11152/).
- 1980s: A Clarksville bank failed on bad farm and energy loans (https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/clarksville-tx-red-river-county).
- 1979: 20.5% of county families were below poverty; in 1981 the county ranked 233rd of 254 Texas counties in per capita income (https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/red-river-county).
- December 2014: East Texas Medical Center Clarksville closed, leaving the town without a hospital; the operator cited low use and Medicare and Medicaid cuts (https://www.ketr.org/news/2014-08-07/clarksville-could-lose-hospital; https://www.pressreader.com/usa/texarkana-gazette/20170717/281522226137624). Whether a replacement hospital opened: UNCONFIRMED.
- 2000 to 2020: Clarksville city fell from 3,883 to 2,857 people (https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/clarksville-tx-red-river-county).
