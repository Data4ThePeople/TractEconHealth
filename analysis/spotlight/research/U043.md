# U043: Northeast Rochester, NY (Joseph Ave, North Clinton Ave, Marketview Heights)

## Name and place
- Northeast Rochester, Monroe County, NY: the **Joseph Avenue / North Clinton Avenue corridor** (old Seventh Ward), with **La Avenida / El Camino**, **Upper Falls** and **North and South Marketview Heights**. OpenStreetMap reverse geocodes of tract centroids give La Avenida (tracts 7, 52), North Marketview Heights (16, 55), Upper Falls (92) and South Marketview Heights (93.01). 1 Joseph Ave geocodes to 93.01 and 640 Joseph Ave to 52 (Census geocoder); 1355 Clifford Ave geocodes to 16.
- Hoeltzer Street (tract 7) and the Clinton-Clifford intersection are the center of the corridor's open-air drug market (https://www.wxxinews.org/post/human-chain-north-clinton-ave-attempt-disrupt-drug-sales-neighborhood).
- No prison, base or college found. Public housing in the tracts not yet checked.

## Rule-out flags
- None. Tracts stable since 1980.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1930s: Rochester HOLC map; the Seventh Ward (then a Jewish and immigrant district) expected to be graded C/D (UNCONFIRMED). https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining
- 1950s-1960s: Housing discrimination confined Black residents to the Third and Seventh Wards; Black unemployment was over 10%. https://rochistory.com/blog/?p=3746 ; https://rocwiki.org/1964_Race_Riot
- July 24-26, 1964: The uprising began at a street dance on Joseph Avenue, spread through the Seventh Ward, and then to the Third Ward; 4 dead, about 350 injured, nearly 1,000 arrested. https://spiritofthepythodd.digitalscholar.rochester.edu/july-1964 ; https://rocwiki.org/1964_Race_Riot ; https://www.wxxinews.org/post/lessons-july-64-new-vision-joseph-avenue
- 1982 onward: Kodak's local employment peaked at more than 60,000 in 1982 and fell steadily; Kodak cut 18,000 Rochester jobs from 1984 to 1990, and had 24,640 by 2002. https://rbj.net/2005/04/08/kodak-loses-title-as-top-employer/ ; https://www.wsws.org/en/articles/2013/09/03/blny-s03.html ; https://rbj.net/2002/10/11/fifteen-years-that-reshaped-rochester-business/
- 2000-2013: Area employment fell by 18,185 from Kodak and Xerox layoffs. https://www.wsws.org/en/articles/2013/09/03/blny-s03.html
- 2010s-2021: The Clinton-Clifford area had the highest concentration of overdoses in Rochester and Monroe County year after year; Project CLEAN targeted the open-air heroin market; outdoor share of overdoses fell from 54% (2019) to 25% (2021). https://www.rit.edu/liberalarts/sites/rit.edu.liberalarts/files/documents/our-work/Disrupting%20the%20Open-Air%20Herion%20Market%20in%20Rochester%2C%20NY.pdf ; https://www.rit.edu/liberalarts/sites/rit.edu.liberalarts/files/docs/CRIM%20Resources/2020%20overdose%20data%20summary%20-%20CLEAN%20TA.pdf
- 2024: Rochester ranked No. 2 Asthma Capital. https://aafa.org/report-names-most-challenging-u-s-cities-for-living-with-asthma-in-2024/

## Story strength
5. The 1964 uprising began on these blocks, the region's one-company economy shed tens of thousands of jobs with dated counts, and the same corridor became the county's overdose center, each with a data series.

## What makes it distinct
A company town without a smokestack collapse: the film and copier jobs left slowly, and the blocks where Rochester's 1964 uprising started became a regional heroin market.
