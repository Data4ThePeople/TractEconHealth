# R039: Austin, Indiana (Scott County)

## Name and place
Tract 18143966800 is the town of Austin in northern Scott County, Indiana, about 30 miles north of Louisville. The tract centroid (38.7496, -85.8141) falls on Austin, and the tract covers 4.8 sq mi. Population about 3,690 (ACS); 3,777 in the 2020 count.

## Rule-out flags
- No prison, college or base (no group quarters in 2020).
- Single tract, small population, so the numbers are noisy.
- Risk that it reads as one event (the 2015 HIV outbreak). The story only works if the job losses and drug use that came before the outbreak are documented.

## History
- **1899:** The Austin Canning Company, later Morgan Foods, was founded and became the town's main employer ([City of Austin history](https://cityofaustin.in.gov/history/morgan-foods)).
- **1986:** The American Can plant next to Morgan Foods closed after more than 50 years. Workers left and poverty rose among those who stayed ([Daily Herald, 2015](https://www.dailyherald.com/?p=211301)). Job count UNCONFIRMED.
- **2000s:** Of the town's three main plants (Pepsi, Tri-Hawk Automotive and Morgan Foods), some closed or cut jobs. Dates and counts UNCONFIRMED ([Daily Herald](https://www.dailyherald.com/?p=211301)).
- **2010s:** Injection of prescription opioids, often Opana, spread. Accounts note drug use going back generations in some families ([LWTech reader excerpting Wapner](https://openwa.pressbooks.pub/lwtech88readings/?p=199)).
- **2014:** About one in four residents lived below the poverty line, and fewer than 10 percent had a college degree ([HuffPost](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/a-touch-of-hope_n_58d1772ee4b07112b647327e)).
- **2015:** An HIV outbreak tied to injection drug use. On March 26 the state declared a public health emergency with 55 confirmed cases. By April 2016 there were 188 confirmed cases, in a county that usually saw about five new HIV cases a year ([Indiana State Dept. of Health release](https://secure.in.gov/health/hiv-std-viral-hepatitis/files/April_10_PR_HIV_Outbreak_April_10.pdf); [Indianapolis Star via AHF](https://www.aidshealth.org/2016/04/indianapolis-star-indiana-town-recovering-190-hiv-cases/); [Peters et al., NEJM, via PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6148928)).
- **2015:** The state allowed an emergency syringe exchange in Scott County, after earlier state law had barred one. Date details UNCONFIRMED in this search.

## Story strength
4. The 2015 outbreak is one of the best-documented public health events in rural America. The decades of job loss before it are thinner and need more dates and job counts.

## What makes it distinct
A cannery town where factory jobs thinned out, injection drug use took hold, and HIV followed. It is the only candidate in this batch where an infectious disease outbreak marks the low point.
