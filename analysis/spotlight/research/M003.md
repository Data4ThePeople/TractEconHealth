# M003: South and east Moultrie, Colquitt County, GA

## Name and place
Colquitt County tract 9707.02 (1.3 sq mi, ZIP 31768), inside the city of Moultrie, on its south and east side (tract bounding box; Census geocoder). Local name UNCONFIRMED. Population 2,323 (ACS); in 2020, 1,009 Black and 630 Hispanic of 2,291 residents. Colquitt County's large vegetable farms use many seasonal farmworkers (UNCONFIRMED for this tract).

## History
- **1914-1917:** Moultrie Packing Company opened in 1914; Swift and Company bought it in 1917 and it was a major employer for decades ([City of Moultrie](https://www.moultriega.com/things-to-do/attractions-activities/swift-company/); [Digital Library of Georgia](https://dlg.usg.edu/record/dlg_vang_clq089)).
- **1940s:** Moultrie had about 16,000 people, about 4,000 of them Black; Spence Field trained pilots in World War II ([Moultrie Observer](https://moultrieobserver.com/?p=269538); [Colquitt County history](https://colquittcountyga.gov/residents/history/)).
- **1960s-1980s:** A Black neighborhood called Rat Row existed in Moultrie until the 1980s ([Moultrie Observer](https://moultrieobserver.com/?p=269538)). Whether it was in this tract is UNCONFIRMED.
- **1996:** Premium Pork, which had bought the Swift plant, closed it ([Times-Enterprise](https://timesenterprise.com/tag/swift-and-co)). A later processor reopened it; National Beef now runs a plant in Moultrie ([Times-Enterprise](https://timesenterprise.com/?p=172185)).
