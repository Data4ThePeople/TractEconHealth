# U001: Northwest One / Sursum Corda area, Washington, DC

## Name and place
- **Northwest One** (the former Sursum Corda area, beside NoMa), Washington, DC. One tract (47.04), 0.2 sq km, 1,721 people. 1140 North Capitol St and 33 K St NW geocode to this tract (Census geocoder); 901 New Jersey Ave NW is in neighboring 47.02.
- The area's housing includes Tyler House Apartments, Sibley Plaza and the Turnkey; Sibley Plaza and the Turnkey are DC Housing Authority owned, and Tyler House and DeSeverna contain many DCHA-subsidized units. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sursum_Corda_Cooperative (which of these sit inside 47.04 is UNCONFIRMED)

## Rule-out flags
- **Stability 0.0**: a piece of a larger 1990 tract; no clean time series.
- **Subsidized housing concentration**: a tiny tract that is likely made up mostly of a few subsidized and public housing buildings, inside one of the fastest-gentrifying parts of the city. The numbers likely reflect who those buildings serve.
- Rule out.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1965: Plans for the Sursum Corda low-income cooperative began. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sursum_Corda_Cooperative
- January 2004: Two killings in Temple Court and Sursum Corda led to the Northwest One redevelopment plan (1,630 units). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sursum_Corda_Cooperative
- 2008: Most participating buildings demolished; residents dispersed. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sursum_Corda_Cooperative
- 2009-2011: Walker-Jones campus, library and Severna Apartments opened. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sursum_Corda_Cooperative
- November 2018: Sursum Corda demolition began for a 1,100-unit project. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sursum_Corda_Cooperative
