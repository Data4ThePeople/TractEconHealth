# U029: Flour Bluff (part), Corpus Christi, Texas. Not Hillcrest.

## Name and place
- One tract, 48355003004. About 1.4 square miles inside the city of Corpus Christi, 2020 population 2,904, 38% Hispanic (2020 PL 94-171, https://api.census.gov/data/2020/dec/pl).
- Centroid 27.674, -97.280; tract bounding box -97.297 to -97.231 longitude, 27.635 to 27.682 latitude (TIGERweb, https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov). That is the Flour Bluff area on the south side of the city, next to Naval Air Station Corpus Christi.
- This is NOT Hillcrest. Hillcrest, beside refinery row, is on the north side of the city near the ship channel, about 12 to 15 miles northwest of this tract.

- June 11, 1940: Congress authorizes naval air stations, the largest in the Flour Bluff section of Corpus Christi (U.S. Naval Institute, https://www.usni.org/magazines/naval-history-magazine/2013/may/corpus-christis-university-air).
- March 12, 1941: NAS Corpus Christi dedicated (Navy cultural resources flier, https://cnrse.cnic.navy.mil/Portals/83/nas_corpus_christi/Documents/Environmental_Support/Cultural%20Resources%20Flier.pdf).
