# U049: Warrendale and the Warren Ave corridor (Warrendale, Warren Ave Community, We Care Community), west Detroit, MI

## Name and place
Eight Detroit tracts on the far west side, population about 31,075, bordered by Dearborn and Dearborn Heights. Detroit neighborhood layer: Warrendale (5457 to 5460), Warren Ave Community (5455, 5456, 5472), We Care Community (5358) (https://services2.arcgis.com/qvkbeam7Wirps6zC/arcgis/rest/services/Current_City_of_Detroit_Neighborhoods/FeatureServer/0). Warrendale borders Dearborn on two sides (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warrendale,_Detroit).

## Rule-out flags
- Tract 5472 holds Gardenview Estates, the mixed-income rebuild of Herman Gardens (about 600 units; HUD layer: https://services.arcgis.com/VTyQ9soqVukalItT/arcgis/rest/services/Public_Housing_Developments/FeatureServer/0). That is one tract of eight, so not disqualifying for the cluster.
- Thin documentation of decline. Income percentile is the least extreme of the Detroit candidates (1.9).
- Boundaries stable (1.0).

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- Early 1900s: Village of Warrendale votes to join Detroit for water service (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warrendale,_Detroit). Year UNCONFIRMED.
- 1939: HOLC grades much of the area "C" (46%), with "D" pockets (17%) and a third ungraded (Mapping Inequality, our point sample).
- 1943: Herman Gardens opens with 2,144 units at Joy Road and the Southfield Freeway (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herman_Gardens).
- Early 1980s: Drug trade problems in Herman Gardens (same source).
- 1985 and 1992: Leslie Elementary closes; in 1992 residents protest the move of Malcolm X Academy into the building and police escort students (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warrendale,_Detroit).
- 1996 to 2009: HUD approves demolition of 685 Herman Gardens units in 1996; Gardenview Estates opens in 2009 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herman_Gardens).
- 2000s to now: Arab American, mostly Iraqi and Yemeni, residents grow in number; a project report cites 46% poverty (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warrendale,_Detroit; https://yemeniamerican.com/en/?p=4873). Report date UNCONFIRMED.

## Story strength (1-5, with one sentence why)
2. Herman Gardens is well documented, but the wider decline of Warrendale has few dated, sourced events.

## What makes it distinct
A late-built, once middle-class edge of Detroit on the Dearborn line that turned over to Black and then Arab immigrant families.
