# M013: North end of Clinton, Iowa

## Name and place

One tract, 19045000100, Clinton, Clinton County, Iowa, on the Mississippi River near 1st Ave. on the city's north side, between downtown and the old town of Lyons (merged into Clinton in 1895). Exact neighborhood: UNCONFIRMED. 2020 census population 2,641; 23 in correctional group quarters, 61 group quarters total. No college or base.

## History (dated bullets with sources)

- **1850s to 1900: lumber boom and bust.** Lyons and Clinton were major sawmill centers until the northern forests were cut out and the sawmills closed by about 1900 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinton,_Iowa).
- **1965 to 1981: floods.** The river crested at 24.85 feet in 1965; an 8.1-mile levee was finished in June 1981 (same page).
- **1970: peak.** Clinton peaked at 34,719 and fell to 24,469 by 2020, a 30% drop (same page, from census).
- **Plant closures** (corn processing, chemical and steel plants): UNCONFIRMED; not found in sources read.
