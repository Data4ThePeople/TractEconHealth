## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1904: The C&O built its line from Huntington to Logan; the Logan coalfield boomed from 1904 to 1929 (e-WV, https://www.wvencyclopedia.org/entries/1399). The C&O kept its rail yard and engine shops for the district at Peach Creek (https://www.wikipedia.com/wiki/Logan_Coalfield; https://brandonraykirk.com/2022/12/12/the-co-shops-at-peach-creek-wv-1974/).
- September 1921: Battle of Blair Mountain, an armed miners' march turned back by the U.S. Army (e-WV, above).
- 1940: Coal employment peaked at about 11,000 miners; 1,146 in 2001, a 90% drop (e-WV, above).
- 1950: County population peaked at 77,391; 32,570 in 2020, a 58% drop (e-WV, above).
- March 8, 1960: Holden 22 mine fire killed 18 (e-WV, above).
- February 26, 1972: The Pittston coal-waste dam on Buffalo Creek failed, killing 125 and destroying a dozen communities in the county (e-WV, above). Buffalo Creek is outside this tract.
- 2005 to 2021: Direct coal jobs about 1,350 at both ends (Marshall University CBER Logan County plan, https://www.marshall.edu/cber/files/2022/08/2022_07_25-Logan_County_Strategic_Plan.pdf).
- Opioid shipments to Logan County: UNCONFIRMED in this pass; data exist in DEA ARCOS (link below).

## What makes it distinct
The rail-yard side of coal: a C&O yard community that existed to move the Logan coalfield's coal, and shrank as mine jobs fell 90% from their 1940 peak.
