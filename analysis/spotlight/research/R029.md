# R029: Hearne, Robertson County, TX

## Name and place
Single tract 48395960501 (Census Tract 9605.01), Robertson County, about 31.7 square miles, population about 3,176. TIGERweb extent: lat 30.83 to 30.947, lon -96.705 to -96.563, which covers most of the town of Hearne (city center about 30.88, -96.59) and land to its west (Hearne CCD). Hearne had 4,544 people in 2020 (https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/hearne-tx). No prison, college or base found; the WWII POW camp west of town closed in 1946 (same source).

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1852: The Hearne family acquired 10,000 acres for cotton plantations (https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/hearne-tx).
- 1868 to 1870: The Houston and Texas Central reached Hearne in April 1868; the International-Great Northern crossed it in 1870, making the town a rail junction and regional cotton market (same source).
- 1942 to 1946: A camp for up to 8,000 German POWs operated just west of town (same source).
- 1960 to 2000: Population peaked at 5,418 in 1980 and fell to 4,690 by 2000 (Texas Almanac table in the same source). Causes of decline are not given there: UNCONFIRMED.
- November 2000: A drug sweep arrested 27 to 28 residents, nearly all Black, on crack cocaine charges based on one informant; cases fell apart and the ACLU sued in 2002 (Kelly v. Paschall) (https://www.aclu.org/documents/stories-aclu-clients-swept-hearne-drug-bust-november-2000; https://www.texasobserver.org/1935-bringing-justice-to-hearne-residents-and-the-national-aclu-team-up-to-bring-justice-to-hearne/). The case was later settled (terms UNCONFIRMED).
- April 2019: Union Pacific stopped building its $550 million Brazos Yard near Hearne and later wrote down half its value (https://www.trains.com/?p=86436). How many local jobs were expected: UNCONFIRMED.
- Rail job losses in Hearne itself over the 20th century: UNCONFIRMED (no source found).
