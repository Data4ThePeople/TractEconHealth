# M006: Somerset borough, Somerset County, PA

## Name and place
- **Somerset** borough (county seat), Somerset County, PA. One tract (211), 2.88 sq km, 1,782 people. Somerset Hospital (225 S Center Ave) geocodes into this tract (Census geocoder).
- Prisons checked: SCI Somerset (1590 Walters Mill Rd) geocodes to tract 208.02, outside. SCI Laurel Highlands (5706 Glades Pike) did not geocode; it is west of town and likely outside (UNCONFIRMED).

## Rule-out flags
- **Thin story**: the borough's population has been flat (5,936 in 1950, 6,454 in 1990, 6,048 in 2020); there is no documented decline in the town itself. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset,_Pennsylvania
- The tract holds the hospital and likely senior and subsidized housing near the county seat's core (UNCONFIRMED), which could pull health and income down by themselves.
- Rule out unless the county coal story is reframed around a mining town rather than the county seat.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1930-1960: Borough grew from 4,395 to 6,347. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset,_Pennsylvania
- 1960-2020: Population roughly flat near 6,000-6,800. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset,_Pennsylvania
- 2002: Quecreek mine flooding outside Somerset; all nine miners rescued. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset,_Pennsylvania
