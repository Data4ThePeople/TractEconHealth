# U045: South Shreveport tract off Mansfield Road (Linwood Avenue and West 70s-80s streets)

## Name and place
Caddo Parish, Shreveport. One tract (22017023701), about 1.2 square miles, bounded roughly by 32.425 to 32.442 N and 93.785 to 93.763 W. Streets in the tract include Linwood Avenue, West 71st through 86th streets, Union Avenue, Wallace Avenue, Valley View Drive and US 171 (Mansfield Road) along one side (TIGERweb road layer). ## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1930: United Gas moves its headquarters to Shreveport; the city is a national oil and gas center. Population grows from 76,655 (1930) to a peak of 206,989 (1980). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shreveport,_Louisiana
- Western Electric's Shreveport plant (payphones) reaches about 7,500 workers at its peak and closes in 2001 after several changes of ownership. Location on Mansfield Road near this tract: UNCONFIRMED. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shreveport,_Louisiana
- August 2012: General Motors' Shreveport plant closes permanently. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shreveport,_Louisiana
- 1980 to 2020: Shreveport loses 9.4% of its population (206,989 to 187,593). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shreveport,_Louisiana
