## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1888 to 1893: Middlesboro was founded (post office 1888, incorporated 1890) by British-financed developer Alexander Arthur as an iron and steel city. A fire in 1890, poor ore and the Panic of 1893 ended the boom by 1893 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middlesboro,_Kentucky).
- 1896: The L&N railroad bought the local line (same source).
- 1950: Middlesboro population peaked at 14,482; 9,405 in 2020, a 35% drop (same source, census table).
- 1970s: Coal revived and the city "prospered again" (same source).
- June 2011: A flash flood after 8.5 inches of rain in 48 hours killed two residents (same source).
