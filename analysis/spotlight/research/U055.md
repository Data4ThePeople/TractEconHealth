# U055: Flint's west side (Civic Park, the Chevy in the Hole and Kettering area, and the Flint Township edge)

## Name and place
Six tracts in the city of Flint (5, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15) plus two just outside the city line (105.01 and 108.12, likely Flint Township or Mount Morris Township). From tract centroids, the cluster runs from Civic Park in the northwest down to the Chevrolet Avenue and Kettering University area near the former Chevy in the Hole plant. Neighborhood names by tract are approximate. Population 15,529.

## Rule-out flags
- **Too similar to U013** (east-side Flint). Both share Flint's GM collapse and the water crisis. U013 is stronger and has lower income.
- Tract 14 has 36 people in correctional facilities and tract 15 has 36 juveniles and 22 in college housing (Kettering). These numbers are small.
- Two tracts are outside the city, which mixes in suburban-edge history.

## History
- **1919:** GM's Modern Housing Corporation built Civic Park for its workers. Sources say 600 to 950 homes were built within months. It was explicitly racially segregated, and most homes cost more than an average hourly worker could pay ([Michigan historical marker, HMdb](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=180450); [Flint Flash, U-M](https://flintflashproject.umich.edu/explore-flash/civic-park)).
- **1936-1937:** The Flint Sit-Down Strike took place at the Chevy in the Hole complex ([Wikipedia, Civic Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civic_Park_Historic_District)).
- **1937:** HOLC graded Flint. Grades for Civic Park and the west side are UNCONFIRMED; check Mapping Inequality.
- **1960s-1980s:** Civic Park went from all white to mostly Black as white families left for suburbs. Dates UNCONFIRMED; the tract race series from NHGIS can show it.
- **1978-2010:** GM employment in Flint fell from about 80,000 to under 8,000 ([Wikipedia, Flint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flint,_Michigan)).
- **1980s-2000s:** The Chevy in the Hole plants closed and were torn down. The site is now Chevy Commons park ([Wikipedia, Civic Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civic_Park_Historic_District)). Closure years UNCONFIRMED.
- **April 2014 to October 2015:** The Flint water crisis ([NBC News timeline](https://www.nbcnews.com/storyline/flint-water-crisis/bad-decisions-broken-promises-timeline-flint-water-crisis-n499641)).
- **2000s-2010s:** Mass vacancy and demolition in Civic Park ([Next City](https://nextcity.org/urbanist-news/flint-anthology-new-book-excerpt); [Flintside](https://flintside.com/?p=8217)).

## Story strength
4. Civic Park is well documented as company housing that emptied out after the company did, but most of its arc repeats U013's.

## What makes it distinct
GM built Civic Park in 1919 for its workers, sold it to white buyers only, and later left. The company town's houses outlived the jobs they were built for.
