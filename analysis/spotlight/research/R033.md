# R033: North Havre and Havre North, Hill County, MT

## Name and place
Hill County tract 403 (11.9 sq mi, ZIP 59501). It covers the north side of Havre and the Havre North CDP, north of the Milk River and the railroad district (Census geocoder; tract bounding box). Population 3,393 (ACS); in 2020, 715 of 3,692 residents (19%) were American Indian, a much higher share than the town as a whole (Chippewa Cree, Gros Ventre and Assiniboine families from Rocky Boy's and Fort Belknap reservations; tribal ties UNCONFIRMED).

## Rule-out flags
- Group quarters 57 (40 college, 17 nursing). Montana State University-Northern may sit near the south edge; not a driver.
- Stable to 1990.
- Thin documentation. Railroad cuts are dated, but the tract's low numbers may reflect who lives on the north side more than the railroad itself.

## History
- **1891:** The Great Northern picked Bull Hook Siding (Havre) as a division point and built locomotive shops there ([Havre Historic Downtown and Railroad District](https://visitmt.com/listing/havre-historic-downtown-and-railroad-district); [Big Sky Treasure](https://sites.google.com/view/bigskytreasure/history/places/havre)). Date from tourism sources; check against the National Register nomination.
- **March 2, 1970:** Great Northern merged into Burlington Northern ([Great Northern Railway history](https://locomotive.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Northern_Railway_(U.S.))).
- **1970s-1990s:** Railroad presence in Havre had been shrinking since the 1970s ([Minneapolis Fed, 1997](https://minneapolisfed.org/article/1997/havre-rail-industry-getting-back-on-track)).
- **1992:** Burlington Northern cut about 190 jobs at the Havre locomotive shop, leaving 50-60; Havre had about 10,000 people ([Minneapolis Fed, July 1992](https://www.minneapolisfed.org/article/1992/havre-faces-effects-of-burlington-northern-cuts)).
- **1997:** The shop reopened with about 100 workers to maintain 163 new GE locomotives ([Minneapolis Fed, 1997](https://minneapolisfed.org/article/1997/havre-rail-industry-getting-back-on-track)).
- **Today:** BNSF ranks behind Northern Montana Health Care as Havre's top employer ([Missoula Current](https://missoulacurrent.com/havre-economic-stability)).
- Farm consolidation on the Hi-Line wheat economy is a likely second cause (UNCONFIRMED; Census of Agriculture can show it).
