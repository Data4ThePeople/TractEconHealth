# U041: Rayville, Richland Parish

## Name and place
One tract (22083970500) covering the town of Rayville, parish seat of Richland Parish in the northeast Louisiana Delta, about 20 miles east of Monroe. The batch labels it "urban" because of the Monroe commuting area, but it is a small rural town. Population about 4,160. 2020 census: 67% Black, 27% white, 11% of housing units vacant.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1925: The Rhymes Memorial Library opens in Rayville, called the first public parish library in Louisiana. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richland_Parish,_Louisiana
- 1940: Richland Parish peaks at 28,829 people. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richland_Parish,_Louisiana
- 1940 to 2020: The parish falls to 20,043 (down 30.5%). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richland_Parish,_Louisiana
- 2000 to 2020: Rayville falls from 4,234 to 3,347, down nearly 21%. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rayville,_Louisiana
- 2024: State officials clear the way for Meta's Hyperion data center on about six square miles of mostly abandoned former rice and soybean farms in Richland Parish; Wikipedia reports some residents saw rents rise six-fold. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richland_Parish,_Louisiana
