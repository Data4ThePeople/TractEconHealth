# U018: Central and South Alexandria (downtown edge, I-49 corridor, and south side)

## Name and place
Rapides Parish, Alexandria (two tracts, 131 and 139, also touch Pineville across the Red River). Tracts:
- 139: downtown edge along the Red River; I-49, US 165/167/71 and the Alexandria-Pineville expressway cross it
- 127 and 128: I-49 and US 167 / 71 corridor, south of downtown
- 110, 120, 129: central Alexandria neighborhoods
- 130: southeast Alexandria
- 131: eastern edge along the river (50% Black, 42% white)

All but 131 are 77% to 94% Black (2020 census). Local neighborhood names (for example the Lower Third or Samtown / Woodside): UNCONFIRMED (web search budget ran out).

Population about 15,570.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1942: The Lee Street riot, racial violence involving Black soldiers in Alexandria's Black business district; witnesses said "as many as 20 people may have been killed," the official report noted 3 soldiers critically injured. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria,_Louisiana
- October 21, 1942: Alexandria Army Air Base opens (later England Air Force Base). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_Air_Force_Base
- 1930 to 1980: Alexandria grows from 23,025 to 51,648. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria,_Louisiana
- October 1990 decision, closed December 15, 1992: England AFB closes under base realignment (BRAC 1991). Job losses: UNCONFIRMED. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England_Air_Force_Base
- May 1, 1996: The 16.6-mile I-49 section through Alexandria, named the Martin Luther King Jr. Highway, opens, completing I-49 in Louisiana. Displacement in Alexandria's Black neighborhoods: UNCONFIRMED. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_49_in_Louisiana
- 1980 to 2020: Alexandria falls from 51,648 to 45,275 (down 12.3%). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria,_Louisiana

## What makes it distinct
A wartime military town whose base closed in 1992 and whose interstate was finished through the city in 1996, the last gap in I-49, so a Black neighborhood story about the military economy leaving and a highway arriving.
