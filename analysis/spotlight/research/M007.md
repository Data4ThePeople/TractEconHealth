## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1884: Marion Steam Shovel Company founded; by 1911 Marion makes about 80% of US steam shovels (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Power_Shovel; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion,_Ohio).
- 1970: City population peaks at 38,646; 35,999 in 2020 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion,_Ohio).
- 1974 to 1978: Marion Power Shovel employment rises from about 1,500 to over 3,200; Dresser buys it in 1977 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Power_Shovel).
- July 23, 1997: Bucyrus buys the company for $40.1 million and later closes the Marion plant; closure year and job count UNCONFIRMED (same source).
- Now: Whirlpool's dryer plant is the largest employer, with about 2,900 workers (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion,_Ohio).
- Opioid epidemic in Marion County: UNCONFIRMED; not researched.
