# U016: Downtown Brockton, MA

## Name and place
- Downtown Brockton (Main Street), Plymouth County, MA. One tract (5109), 0.74 sq km, 2,094 people. Centroid on Main Street per TIGERweb; residents call it "downtown."
- Institutions: the Father Bill's & MainSpring emergency shelter at 54 North Main Street sheltered roughly 130 people a night (https://www.yahoo.com/news/inside-father-bills-dilapidated-shelter-080340815.html). The Census geocoder placed "54 Main St" in this tract; the shelter's tract needs checking against the N. Main address. A new $19.5 million shelter on Manley Street is replacing it (https://news.yahoo.com/father-bills-19-5m-shelter-080059059.html?fr=sycsrp_catchall).

## Rule-out flags
- **Single institution risk:** a 130-bed shelter in a 2,094-person tract could drive the income and health numbers by itself. Downtown senior and subsidized buildings may add to this (UNCONFIRMED).
- One tract only; a downtown core, not a residential neighborhood.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- Late 1800s to early 1900s: Brockton was a national shoe-making center (W.L. Douglas and others). https://www.britannica.com:443/place/Brockton ; https://brockton.gov/residents/about-brockton/
- 1933: Unemployment above 30% (low-quality source, UNCONFIRMED). https://www.brandonjbroderick.com/massachusetts/history-of-brockton-massachusetts
- 1950s onward: Shoe firms moved south and overseas; post-war suburban growth emptied downtown stores. https://www.brandonjbroderick.com/massachusetts/history-of-brockton-massachusetts ; https://brockton.gov/residents/about-brockton/ (plant names, years and job counts UNCONFIRMED)
- 2000s-2020s: Downtown hosts the region's main emergency shelter. https://www.yahoo.com/news/inside-father-bills-dilapidated-shelter-080340815.html
- 2024: Brockton Hospital closed temporarily after a fire (UNCONFIRMED; hospital is outside this tract).
