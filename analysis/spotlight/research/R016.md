# R016: Winnsboro, Franklin Parish

## Name and place
One tract (22041950300) covering the city of Winnsboro, the parish seat of Franklin Parish in the northeast Louisiana Delta. Population about 5,053. 2020 census: 68% Black, 26% white, 11% of housing units vacant.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1920 to 1940: Franklin Parish population doubles from 11,989 (1910) to 24,100 (1920) and peaks at 32,382 in 1940. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_Parish,_Louisiana
- 1940 to 2020: Franklin Parish falls to 19,774 (down 38.9%), the pattern of farm mechanization and out-migration. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_Parish,_Louisiana
- 1970: Franklin Medical Center opens, still the parish's only hospital (39 beds). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnsboro,_Louisiana
- September 14, 1970: Franklin Academy opens as a segregation academy about three weeks after a federal court orders parish schools desegregated. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_Parish,_Louisiana
- 1980 to 2020: Winnsboro falls from 5,921 to 4,862 (down 17.9%); 72.77% Black in 2020. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnsboro,_Louisiana
