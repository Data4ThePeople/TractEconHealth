# U033: Saginaw's east side (Saginaw, Michigan)

## Name and place
Eight tracts in the city of Saginaw, Saginaw County, Michigan: 1, 2, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 13. From tract centroids, seven are on the east side of the Saginaw River, the old city of East Saginaw and the historic center of the city's Black community. Tract 13 is just west of the river. Neighborhood names by tract are approximate. Population 16,294.

## Rule-out flags
- No prison, college or base (group quarters at most 124 per tract, mostly nursing homes; 8 in correctional facilities in tract 2).
- Its story is close to Flint's (GM plants closing). It differs in that a dozen plants closed one at a time instead of one big complex.

## History
- **1889:** Saginaw and East Saginaw merged into one city, and an east-west rivalry lasted into the 2010s ([Wikipedia, Saginaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saginaw,_Michigan)).
- **1918-1919:** GM's foundry complex began (later Chevrolet Saginaw Grey Iron), and Saginaw Malleable Iron opened in 1919 ([Saginaw Library archive](https://archive.saginawlibrary.org/repositories/2/archival_objects/26487); [Foundry Management](https://www.foundrymag.com/uncategorized/gm-phase-out-saginaw-malleable-iron)).
- **1937:** HOLC map of Saginaw. Whether Saginaw was mapped is UNCONFIRMED; check Mapping Inequality.
- **1960:** The city's population peaked at 98,265. In the 1960s and 1970s the city and nearby Buena Vista Township had 12 GM plants ([Wikipedia, Saginaw, from census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saginaw,_Michigan)).
- **1983-1988:** Chevrolet Saginaw Parts closed (1983), Saginaw Steering Gear Plant 1 closed (1984), and Chevrolet Nodular Iron closed (1988) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saginaw,_Michigan)).
- **2001:** Saginaw Steering Gear Plant 2 closed in July. A 1998 report said that shutdown, and possibly the nearby foundry, would affect 2,500 hourly workers ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saginaw,_Michigan); [WSWS 1998](https://www.wsws.org/en/articles/1998/12/sag-d23.html)).
- **May 2007:** Saginaw Malleable Iron closed, with up to 350 workers left ([Foundry Management](https://www.foundrymag.com/uncategorized/gm-phase-out-saginaw-malleable-iron); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saginaw,_Michigan)).
- **July 2009:** Unemployment peaked at 23.5 percent ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saginaw,_Michigan)). In 2013 a federal grant of $11.2 million paid to tear down vacant homes; officials counted about 1,200 to demolish.
- **February 2014:** The TRW plant (the former Chevrolet Saginaw Transmission plant) closed ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saginaw,_Michigan); [AP via Washington Times](https://m.washingtontimes.com/news/2014/feb/14/many-workers-leave-saginaw-trw-plant-for-last-time/)).
- **2020:** The city's population was 44,202, 55 percent below its 1960 peak. Its homicide rate was 50.2 per 100,000, compared with 6.5 nationally ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saginaw,_Michigan)). Check against FBI data.

## Story strength
4. Seven plant closures over 31 years, each dated, plus population, unemployment and violence series. The story is close to Flint's, and the east-side-specific history (renewal, highway, housing) still needs sources.

## What makes it distinct
Saginaw did not lose its jobs all at once. It lost them one plant at a time from 1983 to 2014, and violence rose as the work left. That makes it a story of slow attrition and not a single closing.
