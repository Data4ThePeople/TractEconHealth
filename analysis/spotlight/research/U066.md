# U066: Mattapan, Boston (Gladeside / Morton St area)

## Name and place
- **Mattapan**, Boston, Suffolk County, MA. One tract (1010.02), 1.16 sq km, 6,226 people; centroid near Gladeside Ave (OpenStreetMap reverse geocode of the TIGERweb centroid). Matches editor seed Mattapan. Wellington Hill St geocodes to adjacent tract 1011.01, outside.
- Institutions: none confirmed. Former Boston State Hospital grounds (closed 1979, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattapan) lie nearby; whether any part is in 1010.02 is UNCONFIRMED.

## Rule-out flags
- **Too similar to U064 (Grove Hall)**: the same B-BURG mortgage story, and U064 is deeper on income and health.
- Least extreme in the batch (income 15th, health 16th percentile); median household income about $50,000.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1870: Annexed to Boston. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattapan
- 1968-1970: The most dramatic ethnic transition in Boston's history; blockbusting and B-BURG lending drove out the Jewish community. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattapan ; https://dotnews.com/node/140023
- 1979: Boston State Hospital closed. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattapan
- 1980s: Large Haitian immigration. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattapan
- 2007-2010: Foreclosure wave (UNCONFIRMED for this tract).
