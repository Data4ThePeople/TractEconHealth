# U057: Central, East and Southeast Bakersfield plus Oildale, Kern County, CA

## Name and place
Eight tracts, population about 27,344. Tract internal points (Census geocoder): 4 in Oildale CDP (north of the Kern River); 6.03 in Bakersfield city north of downtown; 13.01 in East Bakersfield CDP and 13.02 in the city (Old Town Kern / East Bakersfield area); 16 in central Bakersfield; 20.01, 20.02 and 21 south of downtown, in the area residents call Southeast Bakersfield (UNCONFIRMED for exact names). No prison, college or base found.

## Rule-out flags
- **Mixed cluster.** Oildale (historically white oil-worker town) and Southeast Bakersfield (historically Black and Latino) have very different histories. The cluster does not hold together as one neighborhood. Consider splitting.
- Boundaries: stable since 1970 (stability 0.873).

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- Early 1900s: Oildale grew next to the Kern River Oil Field, which once produced as much oil as all of Texas; oil camps moved into Oildale (https://www.homes.com/local-guide/oildale-ca/). Dates UNCONFIRMED.
- 1930s: Dust Bowl migrants came to work oil fields and cotton; Bakersfield was hostile to them; Weedpatch Camp was a federal relief camp nearby (https://home.nps.gov/places/weedpatch-camp.htm).
- 1938 to 1950: Racial covenants made East Bakersfield and Southwest Bakersfield subdivisions white-only (Cal State Bakersfield Historical Research Center covenant database, https://hrc.csub.edu/before-1938/). Whether HOLC graded Bakersfield: UNCONFIRMED (not in the main HOLC set as far as found).
- July 21 and August 22, 1952: The Kern County earthquake and an August aftershock heavily damaged brick buildings in Bakersfield; 90 of 264 damaged buildings were torn down (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1952_Kern_County_earthquake).
- 1940s to 1950s: Adelante Vista public housing in East Bakersfield was built on land taken by eminent domain from an older community (https://hrc.csub.edu/housing-history/). Date UNCONFIRMED.
- 1960s to now: Farm labor and oil remain the region's base; the oil price busts of 1986 and 2015 hit Kern employment: UNCONFIRMED (search budget ran out).
- Ongoing: San Joaquin Valley air pollution and oil field emissions: UNCONFIRMED in this session; CalEnviroScreen would show it.

## What makes it distinct
An oil-and-farm-labor city where Dust Bowl migrants, Black families and Mexican American farmworkers each settled into separate, polluted neighborhoods.
