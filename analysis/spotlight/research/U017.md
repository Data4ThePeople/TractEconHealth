# U017: The Mill Creek valley, Cincinnati (West End, Lower Price Hill-Queensgate, East Price Hill, North Fairmount, English Woods, Roll Hill, South Cumminsville-Millvale), OH

## Name and place
Eight tracts, population about 19,310, along the Mill Creek valley and I-75 west of downtown Cincinnati. City neighborhood crosswalk (geomarker-io/cincy, 2020 tracts): West End (2, 269), Lower Price Hill-Queensgate (263), East Price Hill (93), North Fairmount (86.01), Roll Hill (85.02), South Cumminsville-Millvale (77), Westwood (88, which holds the former English Woods site) (https://github.com/geomarker-io/cincy/tree/main/data-raw).

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1940 to 1942: English Woods public housing opens; Sutterview added 1960 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Woods,_Cincinnati).
- 1940s: World War II plants draw Appalachian and Black migrants to Lower Price Hill and the Mill Creek valley; Mill Creek serves as an industrial dumping ground for decades (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Price_Hill_Historic_District; https://soapboxmedia.com/climate-plan-for-lower-price-hill/).
- 1948: City master plan sets up clearance of the West End; in 2023 the city council called it institutional racism and apologized (https://www.wosu.org/2023-06-13/cincinnati-apologize-tearing-down-west-end-urban-renewal).
- 1959 to 1973: Kenyon-Barr clearance razes nearly 3,700 buildings on 450 acres and displaces about 27,000 people, 97% Black, for I-75 and the Queensgate industrial area (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End,_Cincinnati; https://beltmag.com/2020-cincinnati-west-end-displacement/).
- 1950 to 1970: West End population falls from 67,520 to 17,068; 6,824 in 2020 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End,_Cincinnati).
- 2004: Queen City Barrel fire in Lower Price Hill sends benzene, toluene and xylene smoke over the neighborhood (https://soapboxmedia.com/climate-plan-for-lower-price-hill/).
- 2005: Housing authority demolishes about 700 English Woods units; site largely vacant since; English Woods population drops from 4,510 (2000) to 405 (2010) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Woods,_Cincinnati; https://www.wcpo.com/longform/our-forgotten-neighborhoods-english-woods).

## Story strength (1-5, with one sentence why)
5. A dated, well-sourced chain (wartime industry, 1948 plan, Kenyon-Barr clearance, I-75, public housing siting, creek pollution, 2004 fire, 2005 demolitions) with a city apology on record.

## What makes it distinct
The people cleared for I-75 were moved up the same polluted valley into public housing beside the creek and the freeway, and the city has formally apologized for the clearance.
