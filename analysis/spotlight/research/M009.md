## Rule-out flags
- Thin documentation. Tullahoma has grown every decade from 1860 to 2020 (12,242 in 1960 to 20,339 in 2020) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tullahoma,_Tennessee). This is a poor pocket in a growing town, not a place in long decline.
- Boundaries are stable (0.987).

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1924: General Shoe Corporation (later Genesco) starts in Tullahoma. Sporting goods makers (baseballs, bats, golf clubs) also operated there, including Worth and Rawlings (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tullahoma,_Tennessee).
- World War II: Camp Forrest trains infantry and holds prisoners of war (same source; no year given).
- After the war: The Air Force builds AEDC nearby (same source). The 1951 date is UNCONFIRMED.
- Late 20th century: Manufacturing becomes "a smaller part" of the local economy. No closing dates or job counts are given (same source). UNCONFIRMED.
