# R017: Carmi, White County, Illinois

## Name and place
- One tract, 17193958000 (Census Tract 9580, White County), about 3 square miles of land. 2020 population 1,999 (2020 PL 94-171); candidate file pop 2,133.
- The centroid falls in Carmi Township, in and around the city of Carmi, the county seat on the Little Wabash River (Census geocoder, https://geocoding.geo.census.gov). Carmi had 4,865 people in 2020, so this tract is part of the town (Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmi,_Illinois). Which part: UNCONFIRMED.
- Housing: median year built 1951, mostly single-family homes, about 404 renter households of about 1,137 units (ACS 2019-2023, https://api.census.gov/data/2023/acs/acs5).

## Rule-out flags
- Group quarters: 35 people in adult correctional facilities, likely the White County jail (UNCONFIRMED), out of 1,999 (2020 PL 94-171 P5). Not a driver.
- Stable since 1990 (0.911).
- Thin documentation: I found no reporting on plant closings in Carmi itself, and nothing on the decline of the oil boom with dates and job counts.

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1939: Oil found in the Storms and Stinson fields in the Wabash bottoms; workers arrive from Texas and Oklahoma (White County history, https://www.whitecounty-il.gov/history).
- 1930-1960: Carmi grows from 2,932 to 6,152, its peak (census via Wikipedia, above). A secondary source says the town doubled during the oil boom (blog, low reliability, https://drloihjournal.blogspot.com/2016/11/the-history-of-carmi-illinois.html).
- 1960-2020: Carmi falls to 4,865 (minus 21%), with losses in every census but 1980 (Wikipedia, above).
- 2010: The Pattiki coal mine near Carmi (White County Coal, part of Alliance Resource Partners) averages about 220,000 tons a month (DeBruler blog citing SEC filing, https://towns-and-nature.blogspot.com/2022/10/carmi-il-2002-2022-white-county-coals.html?m=0).
- Q4 2016: Pattiki closes permanently, citing the coal market; about 300 workers at closing (Mining Connection, https://miningconnection.com/surface/news/article/alliance_resource_partners_closing_its_pattiki_coal_mine_illinois/; DeBruler blog above; Illinois mines database, https://ilmineswiki.web.illinois.edu/wiki/1040).
- Today: The city says White County is still one of the top three oil-producing counties in Illinois with nearly 1,000 people tied to oil (City of Carmi, https://cityofcarmi.org/?p=125). Not independently confirmed.

## Story strength (1-5, with one sentence why)
2. Two extraction booms (oil in 1939, coal later) and a 2016 mine closing are dated, but the oil decline, the farm side and this tract's own history are poorly documented.

## What makes it distinct
A small county seat built up by an oil boom and later tied to one large coal mine, which closed in 2016.
