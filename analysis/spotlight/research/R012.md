# R012: Claiborne and Union parishes (Homer, Haynesville, Athens, Farmerville, Bernice)

## Name and place
North Louisiana piney hills. Tracts and towns (TIGERweb place layer):
- Claiborne Parish: 9501 (Junction City, Lisbon, part of Homer area), 9502.02 and 9503 (Haynesville), 9504 (Homer), 9505 (Athens)
- Union Parish: 9601 (Marion, part of Farmerville), 9602 (Bernice, Lillie, Spearsville, Junction City), 9604 (Farmerville)

Note: the batch lists the county as Claiborne, but three of the eight tracts are in Union Parish (FIPS 22111).

Population about 21,303. Racially mixed: 23% to 68% Black by tract (2020 census).

## Rule-out flags
- David Wade Correctional Center is in Claiborne Parish near Homer and Haynesville (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claiborne_Parish,_Louisiana), but no included Claiborne tract shows correctional group quarters in 2020, so the prison appears to sit outside this cluster.
- Tract 9604 (Farmerville) holds 150 people in adult corrections (2020 census), about 5% of the tract. Likely a parish detention center, UNCONFIRMED. Not enough to drive the numbers.
- Stability very good (0.933).

## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1921: Oil is discovered at Homer and then at Haynesville. The boom runs into the 1930s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homer,_Louisiana
- 1920 to 1930: Haynesville grows from 903 to 2,541. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haynesville,_Louisiana
- 1930 to 2020: Claiborne Parish falls from 32,285 to 14,170 (down 56.1%), with the steepest drop from 1940 to 1970. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claiborne_Parish,_Louisiana
- 1950 to 2020: Homer falls from 4,749 to 2,747 (down 42.2%). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homer,_Louisiana
- March 24, 2008: Chesapeake Energy announces the Haynesville Shale gas discovery; Haynesville's population still falls from 2,327 (2010) to 2,039 (2020). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haynesville,_Louisiana
- 2010 to 2020: Claiborne Parish falls from 17,195 to 14,170; 18.8% of housing units vacant in 2020. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claiborne_Parish,_Louisiana
- Cotton farming, timber and sawmill decline, poultry plants in Union Parish: dates and job counts UNCONFIRMED.

3. The 1921 oil boom and a 56% population loss since 1930 are well documented with census and production data, but the timber and farm chapters are not yet sourced.

## What makes it distinct
Two oil booms a century apart: a 1921 oil strike that built Homer and Haynesville, and the 2008 Haynesville Shale gas boom that did not stop the towns from shrinking.
