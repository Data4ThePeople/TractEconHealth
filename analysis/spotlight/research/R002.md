## History (dated bullets with sources)
- 1807: Clay County formed; the area's early economy was salt making (salt not confirmed in the source pulled; UNCONFIRMED) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay_County,_Kentucky).
- Coal has been the historical driver of Knox County's economy (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knox_County,_Kentucky); dates UNCONFIRMED.
- 2000: 34.8% of Knox County residents were below the poverty line (same source).
- July 2010: The Washington Post named Clay County the unhealthiest county in Kentucky (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay_County,_Kentucky).
- November 30, 2013: Lexington Herald-Leader, "Decades of poverty and vote-buying led to widespread corruption in Clay County" (cited at the same source).
- June 2014: The New York Times ranked Clay County last among U.S. counties, "the hardest place to live" (same source).
