## Section 3 claims checked

1. "Owsley never had a big employer." **CHANGED.** No source covers all of the county's history. The ag plan supports the present: "Owsley County has no large manufacturing plants and many Owsley Countians drive 25 minutes or more to work each day." Suggested: "Owsley has no large manufacturing plants, and many residents drive 25 minutes or more to work, the county ag council says." (Ag plan, p. 1)
2. "Families lived on small farms, a tobacco allotment and wages earned in other counties, mostly coal." **CHANGED.** "Mostly coal" is not supported. Suggested: "Families lived on small farms and tobacco, and starting in the 1960s and 1970s many men worked in coal mines in neighboring counties." (Ag plan: "Tobacco has been the mainstay of agriculture in the county"; Al Jazeera, entry 4.)
3. "The population fell by more than two fifths between 1940 and 1970 as people left for work." **VERIFIED.** 8,957 to 5,023, -43.9% (Census county counts); Al Jazeera on post-war migration in search of work.
4. "The federal quota system gave small burley farms a steady cash crop from 1938 on." **CHANGED.** "Steady cash crop" is not in a source. Suggested: "From 1938, federal quotas and price supports set how much tobacco each farm could grow and sell." (Pub. L. 108-357 sec. 611; 70 Fed. Reg., Apr. 4, 2005.)
5. "The 2004 law ended it and paid holders out over ten years." **VERIFIED.** FR: "$7 per pound of eligible quota, to be paid in equal installments over 10 years."
6. "The county ag council says the loss of tobacco income accounts for most of the county's lost farm income." **VERIFIED.** Ag plan: "This accounts for the majority of the county's loss of farm income."
7. "By 2017, the county's farms together lost money." **VERIFIED.** NASS 2017 net cash farm income -$199,000; BEA realized net farm income below zero each year 2003-2022.
8. "A resident told Al Jazeera that Walmarts in nearby counties closed local stores." **CHANGED (minor).** He said local businesses "could not survive when Walmart came"; suggested: "A resident told Al Jazeera that local businesses could not survive after Walmarts opened in nearby counties in the 1980s and 1990s."
9. "The rise in pill shipments, the cancer death rate and reliance on public benefits came during the same decades." **CHANGED.** Each piece has a source, but over different spans: pill shipments rose from 308,200 units (2006) to 746,600 (2012) (ARCOS); lung cancer deaths rose 99.7% from 1980 to 2014 (JAMA); transfer receipts grew from 34.9% of income in 1970 to 55.1% in 2009 (BEA CAINC30). Suggested: "Pill shipments rose from 2006 to 2012, lung cancer deaths rose from 1980 to 2014, and government payments grew as a share of income from 1970 on."
10. "The timing lines up, but no source we read ties each one to tobacco." **VERIFIED** as a statement of what the sources do not show. No source opened in this pass links these to the tobacco buyout.
