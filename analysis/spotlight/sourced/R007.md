## Section 3 claims checked

1. "McDowell's economy rested on one product, coal, and for much of the county one buyer, U.S. Steel." **CHANGED.** The coal part is supported (e-WV). "One buyer for much of the county" is not in any source read. e-WV supports: Gary was "for decades West Virginia's most productive coal operation," supplying "the steel mills of the U.S. Steel Corporation." Suggested wording: "McDowell's economy rested on coal, and its largest operation, at Gary, fed U.S. Steel's mills."
2. "The e-WV encyclopedia says mine mechanization after 1950 caused widespread job losses, and the population fell by about half between 1950 and 1970." **VERIFIED.** e-WV quote as in entry 4; census 98,887 to 50,666 is a 48.8% drop (tier A).
3. "When U.S. Steel left Gary in the 1980s, the World Socialist Web Site reported that county personal income 'plunged by two-thirds' that year. That is one outlet's claim and has not been checked against BEA data." **UNSUPPORTED; drop.** Checked against BEA CAINC1 (https://apps.bea.gov/regional/zip/CAINC1.zip, McDowell line 1, thousands of dollars): 1982 $382,388; 1983 $371,674; 1984 $389,440; 1985 $390,734; 1986 $383,651; 1987 $335,034. The largest one-year drop in the 1980s was 12.7% (1986 to 1987). If a number is wanted: "County personal income fell 13% from 1986 to 1987, before adjusting for inflation (BEA)."
4. "The e-WV entry on Welch links the town's population loss to out-migration, lost mining jobs and floods." **VERIFIED** (tier B). Mack H. Gillenwater, "Welch," e-WV, rev. April 14, 2026, https://www.wvencyclopedia.org/entries/938 : population "fell to 3,590 in 2020 due to out-migration. The loss of mining jobs with the mechanization of the coal industry and the devastating floods... contributed to the city's problems."
5. "The timing of the 2001 and 2002 floods lines up with a further drop in population and housing." **VERIFIED** as a timing statement (tier A). Census API: population 27,329 (2000) to 22,113 (2010), down 19.1%; housing units 13,582 to 11,322, down 16.6%. No source ties the drop to the floods.
6. "WV Public Broadcasting reported that the 2016 Walmart closing took away the main grocery store and a key donor to the local food pantry." **VERIFIED** (tier B). WVPB: the food pantry "last year relied on donations from Walmart to help feed 11,000 people."
7. "The heavy pill shipments of 2006-2012 and the county's very short life spans happened after decades of job loss. The timing lines up, but no source we read connects them directly." **VERIFIED** as a timing statement. Update the life span reference to IHME's 2014 figures (entry 14).
