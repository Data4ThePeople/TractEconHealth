# U013 sourced timeline: north-central and east Flint, Michigan

**Summary:** 16 entries checked. All 16 now rest on tier A sources, and every Wikipedia-only figure has been replaced. The biggest changes come from Highsmith's own dissertation. It gives St. John homeownership falling from 32% to 15%, not 50% to 15%. It puts GM's Flint-area workforce at nearly 80,000 in 1954, 33,000 in 1997 and 15,000 by 2002, not 80,000 in 1978 and under 8,000 in 2010. The "deteriorated residence section" quote comes from the 1934 CWA housing survey, not the 1937 HOLC map.

Notes on sources used throughout:
- "MCRC report" = Michigan Civil Rights Commission, *The Flint Water Crisis: Systemic Racism Through the Lens of Flint*, Feb. 17, 2017. Printed page numbers. URL: https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/mdcr/mcrc/reports/2017/flint-crisis-report-edited.pdf
- "Highsmith diss." = Andrew R. Highsmith, *Demolition Means Progress: Race, Class, and the Deconstruction of the American Dream in Flint, Michigan*, PhD dissertation, University of Michigan, 2009. Deep Blue blocks automated access, so I read the Internet Archive copy of the Deep Blue PDF. Printed page numbers. URL: https://hdl.handle.net/2027.42/62230 (archived PDF: http://web.archive.org/web/20241101133630/https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/bitstream/handle/2027.42/62230/ahighsmi_1.pdf). The MCRC report cites this dissertation. The 2015 University of Chicago Press book grew out of it, and its wording or numbers may differ (see pull list).
- "FWATF report" = Flint Water Advisory Task Force, *Final Report*, March 2016. Page numbers are PDF page numbers. URL: https://www.michigan.gov/-/media/Project/Websites/formergovernors/Folder6/FWATF_FINAL_REPORT_21March2016.pdf
