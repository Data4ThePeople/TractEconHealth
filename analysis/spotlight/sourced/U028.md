# U028 sourced timeline: El Segundo Barrio and Chamizal, El Paso

**Summary:** 18 entries checked. 14 now rest on tier A records, 3 on tier B, none on Wikipedia alone. One entry dropped (I-10) and one reworded (the 2006-2007 downtown plan). Main fixes: the 2000 city poverty rate is 22.2%, not 15.4% (15.4% is Texas); 2019 apparel jobs are 1,310 in BLS QCEW, not 1,130; the 1967 tenement survey and fire dates; and the National Register listing date (Nov. 3, 2021).

Notes on sources used throughout:
- "NR nomination" = National Register of Historic Places Registration Form, *Segundo Barrio Historic District, El Paso County, Texas*, NPS Form 10-900, form dated March 1, 2021, Section 8. Page numbers below are the form's own "Section 8 - Page" numbers, which match the PDF page numbers. The deep file's page numbers for 1916 and Paisano (pp. 117-118) were wrong; the passages are on pp. 122 and 124. URL: https://atlas.thc.texas.gov/NR/pdfs/100006994/100006994.pdf
- "Chamizal NRS" = City of El Paso, *Chamizal Neighborhood Revitalization Strategy*, January 2008. Printed page numbers. URL: https://www.elpasotexas.gov/assets/Documents/CoEP/Community-Development/Neighborhood-Associations/Neighborhood-Revitalization/CHAMIZAL-NRS.pdf
- Many NR passages cite Benjamin Márquez, *Power and Politics in a Chicano Barrio* (1985). I could not open the book; it is listed under "For Eric to pull" as a check on the NR's numbers, not as a gap.
