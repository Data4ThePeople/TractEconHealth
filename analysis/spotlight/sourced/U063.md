## Section 3 claims checked

1. "The *Encyclopedia of Chicago* says University officials and local businessmen organized to keep Black families out of the Washington Park Subdivision, and the 1928 covenant followed." VERIFIED. Seligman, *Encyclopedia of Chicago*, Woodlawn.
2. "University records show it paid part of the cost of defending that covenant in court." VERIFIED. Hagerty (2019), p. 1, citing Steere to the Committee on Community Interests, Apr. 27, 1939, Hutchins Administration Records, Box 58, Folder 6.
3. "The federal appraiser who graded the area in 1939 described the coming racial change and cited the court ruling." VERIFIED. Mapping Inequality, D78 area description.
4. "The neighboring D74 notes say no mortgage money was available north of 63rd Street." VERIFIED. Mapping Inequality, D74 area description.
5. "The 1940 Supreme Court case opened the subdivision to Black families, but Hirsch writes that the end of covenants mostly confirmed changes already under way." VERIFIED. Hirsch, *Encyclopedia of Chicago*, Restrictive Covenants ("ratifying a demographic transformation already underway" is Seligman's wording for the 1940 case; Hirsch's for 1948 is "property already falling into black hands").
6. "The *Encyclopedia* says many of Woodlawn's new residents had been pushed out by redevelopment elsewhere, that most white business owners left after 1968, and that arson destroyed hundreds of empty buildings from 1968 to 1971." VERIFIED. Seligman: "refugees from redevelopment elsewhere in Chicago"; "a reported 362 abandoned buildings."
7. "The *Chicago Tribune* reported in 1996 that ridership on the 63rd Street branch fell by about 78% from 1978 to 1993, and that the city wanted to tear down the stretch east of Cottage Grove to clear land for housing." VERIFIED. Washburn, *Chicago Tribune*, June 6, 1996 (12,600 to 2,800 is a 77.8% drop).
8. "For the drop in population and the low life expectancy in these five tracts, the timing lines up, but no source here ties them directly to the covenant or the HOLC grade." Stated as timing only; no causal claim made.
